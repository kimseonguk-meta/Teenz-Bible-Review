[EN] If it's the head priest who slips up and makes everyone else look guilty by association, he's gotta bring a perfect bull to God as an Absolution-Offering for the sin. He'll bring the bull to the entrance of the main tent in God's presence, lay his hand on the bull's head, and then slaughter it right there before God. Then he's gotta take some of the bull's blood, bring it into the Tent of Meeting, dip his finger in it, and sprinkle some of it seven times before God, right in front of the curtain of the Sanctuary. He has to remove all the fat from the bull—the fat that covers the organs, the two kidneys and the fat around them, and the lobe of the liver. Everything else—the bull's hide, all the meat, its head, legs, organs, and guts—he has to take it all outside the camp to a clean spot where they dump the ashes and burn it all on a wood fire.

It's the Absolution-Offering for the congregation.
