----- EN para 0 [1-4] -----

Aaron is in charge of keeping these lamps burning in front of the curtain that screens The Testimony in the Tent of Meeting from evening to morning continually before God. This is a perpetual decree down through the generations. Aaron is responsible for keeping the lamps burning continually on the Lampstand of pure gold before God.
[EN] God told Moses, "Tell the Israelites to bring you top-quality olive oil for the light, so the lamps can stay lit all the time. Aaron's job is to keep these lamps burning from evening till morning before God, right in front of the curtain that hides the Ark of the Covenant. This is a rule forever, for all your descendants. Aaron is in charge of keeping the lamps on the pure gold lampstand burning before God, always."

----- EN para 2 [10-13] -----
[MSG 10-12] One day the son of an Israelite mother and an Egyptian father went out among the Israelites. A fight broke out in the camp between him and an Israelite. The son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name of God and cursed. They brought him to Moses. His mother's name was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri of the tribe of Dan. They put him in custody waiting for God's will to be revealed to them.
[MSG 13-16] Then God spoke to Moses: "Take the blasphemer outside the camp. Have all those who heard him place their hands on his head; then have the entire congregation stone him. Then tell the Israelites, Anyone who curses God will be held accountable; anyone who blasphemes the Name of God must be put to death. The entire congregation must stone him. It makes no difference whether he is a foreigner or a native, if he blasphemes the Name, he will be put to death.
[EN] So, one day, this guy whose mom was an Israelite and whose dad was Egyptian was hanging out with the Israelites, and he got into a fight with an Israelite man in the camp. During the fight, the son of the Israelite woman blasphemed God's name and cursed. They took him to Moses. (His mom's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, from the tribe of Dan.) They put him in jail, waiting to find out what God wanted them to do.
[KO 10-13] 어느 날, 이스라엘 여자와 이집트 남자 사이에서 태어난 아들이 이스라엘 백성들 사이로 나왔다가, 진영 안에서 어떤 이스라엘 남자랑 싸움이 붙은 거임. 근데 그 이스라엘 여자의 아들이 하나님의 이름을 모독하고 저주를 퍼부었어. 사람들이 그를 모세에게 데려갔지. (그의 어머니 이름은 슬로밋이고, 단 지파 디브리의 딸이었음.) 사람들은 하나님의 뜻이 뭔지 알 때까지 그를 가둬 뒀어.

----- EN para 4 [18-22] -----

[EN] "If someone attacks and kills another person, they must be put to death. If someone kills another person's animal, they have to pay it back—a life for a life. If you injure your neighbor, you get the same injury back: a fracture for a fracture, an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth. Whatever injury they caused, the same will be done to them. So, if you kill an animal, you pay it back, but if you kill a person, you're put to death. There are no double standards; the same rule applies to foreigners and natives alike. I am God, your God."
[KO 18-22] "사람 쳐서 죽인 사람은 무조건 죽어야 돼. 짐승 죽인 사람은 짐승으로 갚아야 해. 생명은 생명으로. 이웃한테 상처 입힌 사람은 자기가 한 그대로 갚아야 돼. 골절은 골절로, 눈은 눈으로, 이는 이로. 남한테 상처 입힌 그대로 자기도 당하는 거야. 짐승 쳐 죽인 사람은 갚아야 하지만, 사람 쳐 죽인 사람은 죽어야 해. 이중 잣대는 없어. 외국인이든 본토인이든 똑같은 법이 적용돼. 나는 너네 하나님, 여호와야."
