----- EN para 0 [1-6] -----
[MSG 1-5] "If your offering is a Peace-Offering and you present an animal from the herd, either male or female, it must be an animal without any defect. Lay your hand on the head of your offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. Aaron's sons, the priests, will throw the blood on all sides of the Altar. As a Fire-Gift to God from the Peace-Offering, present all the fat that covers or is connected to the entrails, the two kidneys and the fat around them at the loins, and the lobe of the liver that is removed along with the kidneys. Aaron and his sons will burn it on the Altar along with the Whole-Burnt-Offering that is on the wood prepared for the fire: a Fire-Gift, a pleasing fragrance to God.
[MSG 6-11] "If your Peace-Offering to God comes from the flock, bring a male or female without defect. If you offer a lamb, offer it to God. Lay your hand on the head of your offering and slaughter it at the Tent of Meeting. The sons of Aaron will throw its blood on all sides of the Altar. As a Fire-Gift to God from the Peace-Offering, present its fat, the entire fat tail cut off close to the backbone, all the fat on and connected to the entrails, the two kidneys and the fat around them on the loins, and the lobe of the liver which is removed along with the kidneys. The priest will burn it on the Altar: a meal, a Fire-Gift to God.
[EN] Alright, so if you're bringing a Peace-Offering and it's from your herd, whether it's a male or female, it's gotta be perfect—no defects. You put your hand on its head, then you sacrifice it right at the entrance of the main tent. The priests, Aaron's sons, will then splash its blood on all sides of the altar. For the fire-gift part for God, you offer up all the fat that covers the guts, the two kidneys with the fat around them, and the best part of the liver. Aaron and his sons will burn all that on the altar on top of the other burnt offering that's already on the fire. It's a special gift, and God loves the smell.
[KO 1-6] 자, 만약에 네가 화목제를 드리는데 소 떼 중에서 바치는 거면, 수컷이든 암컷이든 상관없이 흠이 하나도 없는 완벽한 걸로 골라야 된대. 그 제물 머리에 손을 얹고 회막 입구에서 잡는 거야. 그러면 아론의 아들들인 제사장들이 그 피를 제단 사방에 뿌릴 거래. 그리고 화목제 중에서 하나님께 불로 태워드리는 선물로는, 내장을 덮고 있거나 붙어있는 모든 기름, 두 콩팥이랑 그 주위의 기름, 그리고 콩팥이랑 같이 떼어낸 간의 제일 좋은 부분을 드리는 거임. 아론의 아들들이 이걸 제단 위에서, 이미 불 위에 있는 번제물에 더해서 태울 건데, 이게 바로 하나님이 기분 좋아하시는 향기로운 선물이래.

----- EN para 1 [7-11] -----

Lay your hand on the head of your offering and slaughter it at the Tent of Meeting. The sons of Aaron will throw its blood on all sides of the Altar. As a Fire-Gift to God from the Peace-Offering, present its fat, the entire fat tail cut off close to the backbone, all the fat on and connected to the entrails, the two kidneys and the fat around them on the loins, and the lobe of the liver which is removed along with the kidneys. The priest will burn it on the Altar: a meal, a Fire-Gift to God.
[EN] If your Peace-Offering is from your flock, like a sheep, it also has to be perfect, male or female. If it's a lamb, you bring it to God, put your hand on its head, and sacrifice it in front of the main tent. Aaron's sons will splash the blood around the altar. The fire-gift for God from this offering is all its fat—the whole tail cut close to the spine, all the fat around the guts, the two kidneys with their fat, and the best part of the liver. The priest burns this on the altar. It's like a meal, a fire-gift for God.
[KO 7-11] 만약 하나님께 드리는 화목제가 양 떼 중에서 나오는 거면, 이것도 흠 없는 수컷이나 암컷으로 드려야 해. 어린 양을 바치는 거면, 하나님께 그걸 바치고, 머리에 손을 얹은 다음에 회막 앞에서 잡는 거야. 아론의 아들들이 그 피를 제단 사방에 뿌릴 거고. 이 화목제에서 하나님께 불로 태워드리는 선물은 그 양의 모든 기름인데, 척추뼈 가까이에서 잘라낸 통통한 꼬리 전체, 내장에 붙어있는 모든 기름, 두 콩팥이랑 허리께의 기름, 그리고 콩팥이랑 같이 떼어낸 간의 제일 좋은 부분이래. 제사장이 이걸 제단 위에서 태울 건데, 이게 바로 하나님께 드리는 식사이자 불로 태우는 선물인 거지.

Aaron's sons will throw the blood on all sides of the Altar. [EN] If you're offering a goat, you bring it to God, lay your hand on its head, and sacrifice it in front of the main tent. Aaron's sons will splash the blood on the altar. The fire-gift for God is the fat covering the guts, the two kidneys with their fat, and the best part of the liver. The priest will burn all of it on the altar. It's a meal, a fire-gift that smells amazing to God.
