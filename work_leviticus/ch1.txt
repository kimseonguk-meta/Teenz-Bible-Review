----- EN para 0 [1-2] -----
[MSG 1-2] God called Moses and spoke to him from the Tent of Meeting: "Speak to the People of Israel. Tell them, When anyone presents an offering to God, present an animal from either the herd or the flock.
[EN] So, God hit up Moses from the main tent, the Tent of Meeting, and was like, 'Yo, tell the Israelites the deal. When someone wants to bring an offering to God, it's gotta be an animal from your herd or flock.'
[KO 1-2] 하나님이 회막에서 모세를 불러서 말씀하셨대. '이스라엘 백성한테 가서 전해라. 누구든지 하나님께 예물을 바치려면, 가축 떼나 양 떼에서 동물을 골라 바쳐야 한다고.'

----- EN para 1 [3-9] -----
[MSG 3-9] "If the offering is a Whole-Burnt-Offering from the herd, present a male without a defect at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting that it may be accepted by God. Lay your hand on the head of the Whole-Burnt-Offering so that it may be accepted on your behalf to make atonement for you. Slaughter the bull in God's presence. Aaron's sons, the priests, will make an offering of the blood by splashing it against all sides of the Altar that stands at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting. Next, skin the Whole-Burnt-Offering and cut it up. Aaron's sons, the priests, will prepare a fire on the Altar, carefully laying out the wood, and then arrange the body parts, including the head and the suet, on the wood prepared for the fire on the Altar. Scrub the entrails and legs clean. The priest will burn it all on the Altar: a Whole-Burnt-Offering, a Fire-Gift, a pleasing fragrance to God.
[EN] 'If you're doing a Whole-Burnt-Offering with a bull, you gotta bring a perfect one, no flaws, right to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting so God will be cool with it. You put your hand on its head, and that basically makes it your stand-in to get you right with God. Then, you take care of the bull right there in front of God. Aaron's sons, the priests, will take the blood and splash it on all sides of the Altar at the entrance. After that, you skin the offering and cut it into pieces. The priests will get a fire going on the Altar, lay out the wood, and then arrange all the parts, even the head and the fat, on the fire. Make sure to wash the insides and legs super clean. The priest will then burn the whole thing on the Altar. It's a Whole-Burnt-Offering, a gift made with fire, and it smells amazing to God.'
[KO 3-9] '만약 번제를 소로 드린다면, 흠 없는 수컷을 회막 입구로 가져와서 하나님이 기쁘게 받으시도록 해야 한대. 그 번제물의 머리에 손을 얹으면, 그게 너를 대신해서 죄를 씻어주는 속죄 제물이 되는 거임. 그리고 그 소를 하나님 앞에서 잡는 거야. 아론의 아들들인 제사장들은 그 피를 가져다가 회막 입구에 있는 제단 사방에 뿌릴 거래. 그 다음엔 번제물의 껍질을 벗기고 각을 뜨는 거지. 제사장들은 제단 위에 불을 피우고 나무를 잘 벌여 놓은 다음에, 그 위에 머리와 기름을 포함한 각 뜬 고기 조각들을 올려놓을 거야. 내장이랑 다리는 깨끗하게 씻어야 하고. 제사장은 이 모든 걸 제단 위에서 불태울 건데, 이게 바로 번제, 즉 하나님께 드리는 향기로운 화제인 거임.'

----- EN para 2 [10-13] -----
[MSG 10-13] "If the Whole-Burnt-Offering comes from the flock, whether sheep or goat, present a male without defect. Slaughter it on the north side of the Altar in God's presence. The sons of Aaron, the priests, will throw the blood against all sides of the Altar. Cut it up and the priest will arrange the pieces, including the head and the suet, on the wood prepared for burning on the Altar. Scrub the entrails and legs clean. The priest will offer it all, burning it on the Altar: a Whole-Burnt-Offering, a Fire-Gift, a pleasing fragrance to God.
[EN] 'Now, if your Whole-Burnt-Offering is from your flock, like a sheep or a goat, same deal: a perfect male. You handle it on the north side of the Altar, with God watching. Aaron's sons, the priests, will splash the blood on all sides of the Altar. Then you cut it up, and the priest puts all the pieces, head and fat included, on the wood that's ready for the fire on the Altar. Don't forget to scrub the insides and legs. The priest offers it all up, burning it on the Altar. It's a Whole-Burnt-Offering, a gift made with fire, and it smells amazing to God.'
[KO 10-13] '만약 번제물이 양이나 염소 같은 양 떼에서 나온 거라면, 이것도 흠 없는 수컷으로 드려야 한대. 제단 북쪽에서 하나님 앞에서 잡는 거야. 아론의 아들들인 제사장들은 그 피를 제단 사방에 뿌릴 거고. 각을 뜬 다음에 제사장은 머리와 기름을 포함한 모든 조각들을 제단 위 불타는 나무 위에 잘 배열할 거래. 내장이랑 다리는 깨끗하게 씻고. 제사장은 이 모든 걸 제단 위에서 불태워 바칠 건데, 이것도 번제, 즉 하나님께 드리는 향기로운 화제인 거임.'

----- EN para 3 [14-17] -----
[MSG 14-17] "If a bird is presented to God for the Whole-Burnt-Offering it can be either a dove or a pigeon. The priest will bring it to the Altar, wring off its head, and burn it on the Altar. But he will first drain the blood on the side of the Altar, remove the gizzard and its contents, and throw them on the east side of the Altar where the ashes are piled. Then rip it open by its wings but leave it in one piece and burn it on the Altar on the wood prepared for the fire: a Whole-Burnt-Offering, a Fire-Gift, a pleasing fragrance to God."
  !! numbers possibly missing in EN: ['one']
[EN] 'And if you're bringing a bird for the Whole-Burnt-Offering, it can be a dove or a pigeon. The priest takes it to the Altar, wrings its head off, and burns it on the Altar. But first, he's gotta drain the blood on the side of the Altar, take out the gizzard and whatever's in it, and toss them on the east side of the Altar where they keep the ashes. Then, you rip it open by the wings—but don't tear it completely apart—and the priest burns it on the Altar on the wood. That's a Whole-Burnt-Offering, a gift made with fire, and it smells amazing to God.'
[KO 14-17] '만약 새를 번제물로 하나님께 드린다면, 산비둘기나 집비둘기도 괜찮대. 제사장이 그걸 제단으로 가져가서 머리를 비틀어 끊고 제단 위에서 불사를 거야. 근데 먼저 피는 제단 곁에다 다 흘려버리고, 모이주머니랑 그 안의 내용물은 꺼내서 제단 동쪽 재 버리는 곳에 던져버려야 한대. 그런 다음 날개 쪽을 잡고 찢는데, 완전히 둘로 찢지는 말고, 그 상태로 제단 위 불타는 나무 위에서 불사르는 거임. 이것도 번제, 즉 하나님께 드리는 향기로운 화제인 거지.'
