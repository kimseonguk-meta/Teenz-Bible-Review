----- EN para 0 [1-6] -----
[MSG 1-7] God spoke to Moses: "Speak to Aaron and his sons and all the Israelites. Tell them, This is what God commands: Any and every man who slaughters an ox or lamb or goat inside or outside the camp instead of bringing it to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting to offer it to God in front of The Dwelling of God—that man is considered guilty of bloodshed; he has shed blood and must be cut off from his people. This is so the Israelites will bring to God the sacrifices that they're in the habit of sacrificing out in the open fields. They must bring them to God and the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting and sacrifice them as Peace-Offerings to God. The priest will splash the blood on the Altar of God at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting and burn the fat as a pleasing fragrance to God. They must no longer offer their sacrifices to goat-demons—a kind of religious orgy. This is a perpetual decree down through the generations.
[EN] Alright, so God hits up Moses and is like, 'Yo, tell Aaron, his sons, and all the Israelites the deal. Here's the command from the big guy: If anyone from now on decides to kill an ox, lamb, or goat, whether they're in the camp or not, they *have* to bring it to the entrance of the main tent, the Tent of Meeting, to offer it to God right there in front of His place. If they don't, that's a major foul. Like, they're guilty of murder, for real. They've shed blood and will get kicked out of the community. The whole point is to get the Israelites to stop doing their own thing and sacrificing animals in random fields. From now on, they gotta bring their sacrifices to God, to the priest at the Tent of Meeting, and do it as a proper Peace-Offering. The priest will then splash the blood on God's altar and burn the fat, which is like a super pleasing smell to God. And another thing, they absolutely have to stop sacrificing to those goat-demon things. That's some weird, culty stuff and it's canceled. This rule is forever, for all time.'
[KO 1-6] 하나님이 모세한테 말했대. '아론이랑 아들들, 그리고 모든 이스라엘 백성한테 가서 전해. 이게 하나님 명령이야. 누구든지 소나 양, 염소를 잡을 때, 캠프 안이든 밖이든 상관없이, 무조건 회막 문 앞으로 가져와서 하나님 집 앞에서 제물로 바쳐야 돼. 만약에 안 그러고 딴데서 잡으면, 그 사람은 피를 흘린 죄로 유죄인 거임. 피를 흘렸으니까 백성 중에서 쫓겨나야 돼. 왜냐하면, 이스라엘 사람들이 맨날 들판에서 아무렇게나 제사 지내는 습관을 고치려고 그러는 거야. 이제부터는 무조건 하나님한테, 회막 문 앞에 있는 제사장한테 가져와서 화목제로 드려야 되는 거지. 그러면 제사장이 그 피를 회막 문 앞에 있는 하나님 제단에 뿌리고, 기름은 태워서 하나님을 기쁘게 하는 향기로 만들 거야. 그리고 이제 다시는 염소 귀신한테 제사 지내지 마. 그건 진짜 이상한 종교 축제 같은 거니까. 이건 앞으로 대대로 영원히 지켜야 할 규칙이야.'

----- EN para 3 [12-14] -----
[MSG 10-12] "If any Israelite or foreigner living among them eats blood, I will disown that person and cut him off from his people, for the life of an animal is in the blood. I have provided the blood for you to make atonement for your lives on the Altar; it is the blood, the life, that makes atonement. That's why I tell the People of Israel, 'Don't eat blood.' The same goes for the foreigner who lives among you, 'Don't eat blood.'
[MSG 13-14] "Any and every Israelite—this also goes for the foreigners—who hunts down an animal or bird that is edible, must bleed it and cover the blood with dirt, because the life of every animal is its blood—the blood is its life. That's why I tell the Israelites, 'Don't eat the blood of any animal because the life of every animal is its blood. Anyone who eats the blood must be cut off.'
[EN] “And for any Israelite, or foreigner for that matter, who goes hunting and catches an animal or bird that's good to eat, they have to drain all the blood out and cover it with dirt. It's because the life of every creature is its blood—the blood is its life. That's why I'm telling the Israelites, 'Don't eat the blood of any animal, because the life of every animal is its blood.' Anyone who eats it will be cut off, period.
[KO 12-14] '그리고 이스라엘 사람이든 외국인이든, 사냥해서 먹을 수 있는 짐승이나 새를 잡으면, 반드시 피를 빼고 그 피를 흙으로 덮어야 해. 왜냐하면 모든 동물의 생명은 피고, 피가 곧 생명이니까. 그래서 내가 이스라엘 백성한테 말하는 거야. '어떤 동물의 피도 먹지 마라. 모든 동물의 생명은 피에 있기 때문이다. 피를 먹는 자는 누구든지 끊어질 것이다.'

----- EN para 4 [15-16] -----
[MSG 15-16] "Anyone, whether native or foreigner, who eats from an animal that is found dead or mauled must wash his clothes and bathe in water; he remains unclean until evening and is then clean. If he doesn't wash or bathe his body, he'll be held responsible for his actions."
[EN] “And one last thing. If anyone, whether they're from here or not, eats an animal they just found dead or that was killed by another animal, they need to wash their clothes and take a bath. They'll be considered 'unclean' until the evening, but then they're good. But if they don't wash up, they'll have to face the consequences for their actions.”
[KO 15-16] '마지막으로, 본토인이든 외국인이든, 죽은 채로 발견된 동물이나 다른 짐승에게 찢겨 죽은 동물을 먹은 사람은 옷을 빨고 물로 목욕해야 해. 저녁까지는 부정하지만 그 후에는 깨끗해져. 만약 옷을 빨지 않거나 몸을 씻지 않으면, 자기 행동에 대한 책임을 져야 할 거야.'
