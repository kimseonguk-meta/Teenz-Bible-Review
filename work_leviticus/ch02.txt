----- EN para 0 [1-3] -----
[MSG 1-3] "When you present a Grain-Offering to God, use fine flour. Pour oil on it, put incense on it, and bring it to Aaron's sons, the priests. One of them will take a handful of the fine flour and oil, with all the incense, and burn it on the Altar for a memorial: a Fire-Gift, a pleasing fragrance to God. The rest of the Grain-Offering is for Aaron and his sons—a most holy part of the Fire-Gifts to God.
[EN] When you present a Grain-Offering to God, use the good stuff: fine flour. Pour some oil on it, add some incense, and bring it to Aaron’s sons, the priests. One of them will take a handful of the flour and oil, with all the incense, and burn it on the Altar as a memorial. It's a Fire-Gift, a scent that God really likes. The rest of the Grain-Offering is for Aaron and his sons—it's the most holy part of the Fire-Gifts to God.
[KO 1-3] 하나님께 곡물제물을 바칠 때는, 고운 밀가루를 써야 한대. 거기다 기름을 붓고 향도 좀 뿌려서, 아론의 아들들인 제사장들한테 가져가는 거야. 그럼 그중 한 명이 고운 밀가루랑 기름 한 줌에 향까지 전부 집어서 제단 위에서 태우는데, 이게 기념물이래. 하나님이 완전 좋아하시는 향기로운 불 선물인 거지. 남은 곡물제물은 아론과 그의 아들들 몫인데, 이건 하나님께 드리는 불 선물 중에 제일 거룩한 부분이래.

----- EN para 1 [4] -----
[MSG 4] "When you present a Grain-Offering of oven-baked loaves, use fine flour, mixed with oil but no yeast. Or present wafers made without yeast and spread with oil.
[EN] If you're bringing a Grain-Offering of oven-baked loaves, use fine flour mixed with oil, but no yeast. Or you can offer wafers made without yeast and spread with oil.
[KO 4] 만약 화덕에 구운 빵으로 곡물제물을 바칠 거면, 고운 밀가루에 기름은 섞되 효모는 넣으면 안 된대. 아니면 효모 없이 만든 얇은 과자에 기름을 발라서 드려도 된대.

----- EN para 2 [5-6] -----
[MSG 5-6] "If you bring a Grain-Offering cooked on a griddle, use fine flour mixed with oil but without yeast. Crumble it and pour oil on it—it's a Grain-Offering.
[EN] If your Grain-Offering is cooked on a griddle, use fine flour mixed with oil but no yeast. Crumble it up and pour oil on it—that's a Grain-Offering.
[KO 5-6] 철판에다 구운 곡물제물을 가져올 거면, 그것도 고운 밀가루에 기름 섞고 효모는 빼야 돼. 그걸 부숴서 기름을 부으면, 그게 곡물제물인 거임.

----- EN para 3 [7] -----
[MSG 7] "If you bring a Grain-Offering deep-fried in a pan, make it of fine flour with oil.
[EN] If you're bringing a Grain-Offering that's deep-fried, make it with fine flour and oil.
[KO 7] 만약 기름에 튀긴 곡물제물을 가져올 거면, 고운 밀가루랑 기름으로 만들면 된대.

----- EN para 4 [8-10] -----
[MSG 8-10] "Bring the Grain-Offering you make from these ingredients and present it to the priest. He will bring it to the Altar, break off a memorial piece from the Grain-Offering, and burn it on the Altar: a Fire-Gift, a pleasing fragrance to God. The rest of the Grain-Offering is for Aaron and his sons—a most holy part of the gifts to God.
[EN] Bring the Grain-Offering you've made from these ingredients and give it to the priest. He'll take it to the Altar, break off a memorial piece, and burn it on the Altar. It's a Fire-Gift, a fragrance that pleases God. The rest of the Grain-Offering goes to Aaron and his sons—it's a super holy part of the gifts to God.
[KO 8-10] 이렇게 만든 곡물제물을 제사장한테 가져다주면, 제사장이 제단으로 가져가서 기념으로 조각을 좀 떼서 태운대. 이게 하나님이 좋아하시는 향기로운 불 선물이래. 남은 건 아론이랑 아들들 몫인데, 하나님께 드리는 선물 중에 제일 거룩한 부분이래.

----- EN para 5 [11-13] -----
[MSG 11-13] "All the Grain-Offerings that you present to God must be made without yeast; you must never burn any yeast or honey as a Fire-Gift to God. You may offer them to God as an offering of firstfruits but not on the Altar as a pleasing fragrance. Season every presentation of your Grain-Offering with salt. Don't leave the salt of the covenant with your God out of your Grain-Offerings. Present all your offerings with salt.
[EN] Here's a major rule: All the Grain-Offerings you give to God must be made without yeast. You must never burn any yeast or honey as a Fire-Gift to God. You can offer them to God as a firstfruits offering, but they don't go on the Altar as a pleasing fragrance. And make sure you season every single Grain-Offering with salt. Don't forget the salt of the covenant with your God in your offerings. Present all your offerings with salt.
[KO 11-13] 여기서 완전 중요한 규칙이 있는데, 하나님께 드리는 모든 곡물제물은 효모 없이 만들어야 한다는 거야. 효모나 꿀은 절대 하나님께 불 선물로 태워드리면 안 된대. 첫 수확물로 하나님께 드릴 수는 있지만, 제단 위에서 향기로운 제물로 태우는 건 안 된다는 거지. 그리고 모든 곡물제물에는 소금으로 간을 해야 해. 네 하나님과의 언약의 소금을 제물에서 빼먹으면 안 돼. 모든 제물은 소금이랑 같이 드려야 하는 거임.

----- EN para 6 [14-16] -----
[MSG 14-16] "If you present a Grain-Offering of firstfruits to God, bring crushed heads of the new grain roasted. Put oil and incense on it—it's a Grain-Offering. The priest will burn some of the mixed grain and oil with all the incense as a memorial—a Fire-Gift to God."
[EN] If you bring a Grain-Offering of firstfruits to God, bring crushed heads of new grain that have been roasted. Put oil and incense on it—it's a Grain-Offering. The priest will burn some of the mixed grain and oil with all the incense as a memorial—a Fire-Gift to God.
[KO 14-16] 만약 첫 수확물로 곡물제물을 하나님께 드린다면, 볶은 새 곡식의 이삭을 빻아서 가져와야 한대. 거기에 기름과 향을 더하면, 그게 바로 곡물제물이야. 제사장은 그 빻은 곡식과 기름 일부를 모든 향과 함께 기념물로 태울 건데, 이게 바로 하나님께 드리는 불 선물이래.
