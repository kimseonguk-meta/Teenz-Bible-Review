----- EN para 0 [1-5] -----
[MSG 1-5] God spoke to Moses: "Tell the People of Israel, A woman who conceives and gives birth to a boy is ritually unclean for seven days, the same as during her menstruation. On the eighth day circumcise the boy. The mother must stay home another thirty-three days for purification from her bleeding. She may not touch anything holy or enter the Sanctuary until the days of her purification are complete. If she gives birth to a girl, she is unclean for fourteen days, the same as during her menstruation. She must stay home for sixty-six days for purification from her bleeding.
  !! numbers possibly missing in EN: ['fourteen', 'seven', 'six', 'thirty', 'three']
[EN] So God hit up Moses and was like, 'Yo, tell the Israelites the deal. When a woman has a baby boy, she's considered 'unclean' for a week, kinda like when she's on her period. Then, on day eight, the baby boy gets circumcised. After that, the mom has to chill at home for 33 more days to get purified from all the bleeding. She can't touch any holy stuff or go to the main holy place until she's all good. And if she has a baby girl, she's 'unclean' for two weeks, just like her period. Then she has to stay home for another 66 days to get purified.'
[KO 1-5] 하나님이 모세한테 말했대. '이스라엘 백성한테 전해. 어떤 여자가 임신해서 아들을 낳으면, 7일 동안 부정하게 되는 거야. 약간 생리할 때처럼. 그리고 8일째 되는 날에 아기 할례를 시키는 거지. 엄마는 피 흘린 것 때문에 33일 더 집에서 쉬어야 된대. 정화될 때까지. 그동안은 거룩한 거 만지면 안 되고, 성소에도 들어가면 안 된대. 만약에 딸을 낳으면, 14일 동안 부정하대. 생리할 때처럼. 그리고 66일 동안 집에서 쉬면서 정화해야 되는 거임.'
