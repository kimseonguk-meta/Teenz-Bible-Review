----- EN para 0 [1-6] -----
[MSG 1-6] God spoke to Moses: "When anyone sins by betraying trust with God by deceiving his neighbor regarding something entrusted to him, or by robbing or cheating or threatening him; or if he has found something lost and lies about it and swears falsely regarding any of these sins that people commonly commit—when he sins and is found guilty, he must return what he stole or extorted, restore what was entrusted to him, return the lost thing he found, or anything else about which he swore falsely. He must make full compensation, add twenty percent to it, and hand it over to the owner on the same day he brings his Compensation-Offering. He must present to God as his Compensation-Offering a ram without any defect from the flock, assessed at the value of a Compensation-Offering.
[EN] God hit up Moses and was like, "Yo, if someone messes up and breaks my trust by scamming their neighbor—like if they were supposed to be watching something for a friend and they lie about it, or they straight up rob or cheat someone, or they find something someone lost and then lie and swear they didn't... basically any of the usual ways people sin... if they do that and get caught, they have to give back whatever they stole or scammed. Plus, they gotta add an extra 20% on top and give it all back to the person they wronged. And on that same day, they have to bring a special offering to me, a perfect ram, to show they're sorry.
[KO 1-6] 하나님이 모세한테 말했대. "야, 만약에 누가 하나님과의 신뢰를 깨고 이웃한테 사기 치면, 예를 들어서 친구가 맡긴 물건 떼어먹거나, 강도질하거나, 협박하거나, 아니면 남이 잃어버린 물건 주워놓고 거짓말하고 맹세까지 하면, 그러니까 흔히 저지르는 죄들 있잖아. 그런 죄를 짓고 걸리면, 훔치거나 뺏은 거 다 돌려줘야 된대. 거기다 20% 더 얹어서 피해자한테 바로 그날 줘야 되는 거임. 그리고 속건제물로 흠 없는 숫양 한 마리를 하나님께 바쳐야 한대.

----- EN para 2 [8-13] -----
[MSG 8-13] God spoke to Moses: "Command Aaron and his sons. Tell them, These are the instructions for the Whole-Burnt-Offering. Leave the Whole-Burnt-Offering on the Altar hearth through the night until morning, with the fire kept burning on the Altar. Then dress in your linen clothes with linen underwear next to your body. Remove the ashes remaining from the Whole-Burnt-Offering and place them beside the Altar. Then change clothes and carry the ashes outside the camp to a clean place. Meanwhile keep the fire on the Altar burning; it must not go out. Replenish the wood for the fire every morning, arrange the Whole-Burnt-Offering on it, and burn the fat of the Peace-Offering on top of it all. Keep the fire burning on the Altar continuously. It must not go out.
[EN] God also told Moses, "Give Aaron and his sons the rundown on the burnt offering. It has to stay on the altar all night until the morning, with the fire going the whole time. In the morning, the priest needs to put on his special linen clothes, right down to his underwear, and clean up the ashes from the offering. He puts the ashes next to the altar, then changes his clothes and takes the ashes to a clean spot outside the camp. The fire on the altar has to keep burning, no excuses. Every morning, they need to add more wood, put the offering on it, and burn the fat from the peace offering on top. Seriously, that fire can never go out.
[KO 8-13] 하나님이 모세한테 또 말했대. "아론과 그의 아들들한테 번제에 대한 규정을 알려줘라. 번제물은 아침까지 밤새 제단 위에 두고, 제단 불은 계속 피워놔야 한대. 아침이 되면 제사장은 세마포 옷이랑 속옷을 입고 번제물 타고 남은 재를 치워서 제단 옆에 둬야 함. 그러고 나서 옷을 갈아입고 그 재를 진영 밖 깨끗한 곳으로 가져가야 된대. 그동안 제단 불은 계속 타올라야 하고, 절대 꺼트리면 안 된대. 매일 아침 장작을 새로 넣고, 그 위에 번제물을 올리고, 화목제물의 기름을 태워야 하는 거임. 제단 불은 계속, 절대 꺼지면 안 된대.

----- EN para 3 [14-19] -----
[MSG 14-18] "These are the instructions for the Grain-Offering. Aaron's sons are to present it to God in front of the Altar. The priest takes a handful of the fine flour of the Grain-Offering with its oil and all its incense and burns this as a memorial on the Altar, a pleasing fragrance to God. Aaron and his sons eat the rest of it. It is unraised bread and so eaten in a holy place—in the Courtyard of the Tent of Meeting. They must not bake it with yeast. I have designated it as their share of the gifts presented to me. It is very holy, like the Absolution-Offering and the Compensation-Offering. Any male descendant among Aaron's sons may eat it. This is a fixed rule regarding God's gifts, stretching down the generations. Anyone who touches these offerings must be holy."
[MSG 19-23] God spoke to Moses: "This is the offering which Aaron and his sons each are to present to God on the day he is anointed: two quarts of fine flour as a regular Grain-Offering, half in the morning and half in the evening. Prepare it with oil on a griddle. Bring it well-mixed and then present it crumbled in pieces as a pleasing fragrance to God. Aaron's son who is anointed to succeed him offers it to God—this is a fixed rule. The whole thing is burned. Every Grain-Offering of a priest is burned completely; it must not be eaten."
  !! numbers possibly missing in EN: ['half', 'two']
[EN] And here's the scoop on the grain offering. Aaron's sons have to present it to me right in front of the altar. The priest scoops up a handful of the flour, oil, and incense, and burns it on the altar. It's like a little reminder that smells awesome to me. Aaron and his sons get to eat the rest, but it has to be bread made without yeast, and they have to eat it in a holy place, the courtyard of the Tent of Meeting. No baking it with yeast, that's a hard rule. I've set it aside as their cut of the gifts. It's super holy, just like the offerings for forgiveness. Any guy in Aaron's family line can eat it. This is a forever rule about my gifts. Anyone who touches these offerings has to be holy, for real.
[KO 14-19] 이건 소제에 대한 규정인데, 아론의 아들들이 제단 앞에서 하나님께 바쳐야 한대. 제사장은 소제물인 고운 가루 한 줌이랑 기름, 유향을 전부 가져다가 기념물로 제단에서 불사르는데, 이게 하나님한테는 향기로운 냄새인 거임. 남은 건 아론과 그의 아들들이 먹는데, 누룩 없이 만든 빵이라 거룩한 곳, 바로 회막 뜰에서 먹어야 한대. 절대 누룩 넣고 구우면 안 됨. 이건 내가 그들 몫으로 정해준 거니까. 속죄제나 속건제처럼 완전 거룩한 거임. 아론의 자손 중에 남자는 다 먹을 수 있대. 이건 대대로 내려오는 하나님의 선물에 대한 규칙임. 이 제물에 닿는 사람은 누구든 거룩해야 한대.

[KO 20-23] 하나님이 모세한테 또 말했대. "아론과 그의 아들들이 기름부음 받는 날에 하나님께 바쳐야 할 제물은 이거임. 고운 밀가루 2리터를 아침저녁으로 나눠서 정기적인 소제로 바쳐야 한대. 기름 두른 철판에 잘 섞어서 준비하고, 부서진 조각으로 만들어서 하나님께 향기로운 냄새로 바쳐야 함. 아론의 뒤를 이을 아들도 똑같이 해야 된대. 이건 불변의 규칙임. 전부 다 태워야 하고, 제사장이 바치는 소제는 하나도 남김없이 불살라야 한대. 절대 먹으면 안 됨.

----- EN para 5 [24-30] -----
[MSG 24-30] God spoke to Moses: "Tell Aaron and his sons, These are the instructions for the Absolution-Offering. Slaughter the Absolution-Offering in the place where the Whole-Burnt-Offering is slaughtered before God—the offering is most holy. The priest in charge eats it in a holy place, the Courtyard of the Tent of Meeting. Anyone who touches any of the meat must be holy. A garment that gets blood spattered on it must be washed in a holy place. Break the clay pot in which the meat was cooked. If it was cooked in a bronze pot, scour it and rinse it with water. Any male among the priestly families may eat it; it is most holy. But any Absolution-Offering whose blood is brought into the Tent of Meeting to make atonement in the Sanctuary must not be eaten, it has to be burned."
[EN] Then God said to Moses, "Tell Aaron and his sons the rules for the forgiveness offering. They have to slaughter it in the same place they do the big burnt offering. It's super holy. The priest in charge gets to eat it in a holy place, the courtyard of the Tent of Meeting. Anyone who touches any of the meat has to be holy. If blood splatters on a piece of clothing, it has to be washed in a holy place. If the meat was cooked in a clay pot, you have to smash the pot. If it was a bronze pot, you have to scrub it clean and rinse it with water. Any guy from the priest families can eat it; it's that holy. But if the blood from a forgiveness offering is brought into the Tent of Meeting to make things right in the Sanctuary, no one can eat it. It has to be burned."
[KO 24-30] 하나님이 모세한테 또 말했대. "아론과 그의 아들들한테 속죄제 규정을 알려줘라. 속죄제물은 번제물을 잡는 곳에서 잡아야 하는데, 이건 지극히 거룩한 거임. 담당 제사장은 거룩한 곳, 회막 뜰에서 그걸 먹어야 한대. 그 고기에 닿는 사람은 누구든 거룩해야 함. 피가 튀긴 옷은 거룩한 곳에서 빨아야 하고, 고기를 삶은 토기는 깨뜨려야 된대. 놋그릇이었으면 박박 닦고 물로 헹궈야 함. 제사장 가문의 남자는 누구나 먹을 수 있대. 완전 거룩한 거니까. 근데 성소에서 속죄하려고 회막으로 가져온 속죄제물의 피는 절대 먹으면 안 되고, 불에 태워야 한대."
