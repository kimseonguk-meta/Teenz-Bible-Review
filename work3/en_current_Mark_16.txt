[EN 0] Very early on Sunday morning, Mary Magdalene and two other women bought spices to anoint Jesus's body. They were walking to the tomb, wondering, "Who is going to help us roll that massive stone away?"

---

[EN 1] But when they got there, the stone was already rolled back!

---

[EN 2] They walked into the tomb, and a young man wearing a glowing white robe (an angel) was just sitting there. The women completely freaked out. The angel said, "Don't be terrified. You are looking for Jesus, who was crucified. He isn't here! He has risen from the dead! Look, here is where they put him. Now go tell his disciples—especially Peter—that he is going ahead of you to Galilee, just like he promised."

---

[EN 3] The women ran out of the tomb, totally freaked out and trembling. They didn't say a word to anyone—they were too scared.

---

[EN 4] (Note: The oldest, most original manuscripts of Mark end right here on a massive cliffhanger): The women ran out of the tomb, trembling and completely bewildered. They were so absolutely terrified that they didn't say anything to anyone.

---

[EN 5] (Later additions to Mark wrap up the story): Jesus appeared first to Mary Magdalene, then to two guys walking in the country, and finally to the remaining eleven disciples while they were eating. He gave them their ultimate mission:

---

[EN 6] "Go into the entire world and preach the Good News to absolutely everyone. Anyone who believes and is baptized will be saved—but anyone who refuses to believe will be condemned."

---

[EN 7] "And these insane signs will follow everyone who believes: they'll drive out demons in my name, they'll speak in brand-new languages, they'll pick up snakes with their bare hands, they'll drink poison and walk away totally fine, and they'll lay hands on sick people and heal them."

---

[EN 8] After speaking to them, the Lord Jesus was taken up into heaven and sat down at the right hand of God. The disciples went out and preached everywhere, and the Lord backed up their message with massive power and miracles.
