### TITLE EN: The Magician and the Road Trip
### TITLE KO: 마술사와 로드 트립

[MSG 0] range=1-2 header='Simon the Wizard'
That set off a terrific persecution of the church in Jerusalem. The believers were all scattered throughout Judea and Samaria. All, that is, but the apostles. Good and brave men buried Stephen, giving him a solemn funeral—not many dry eyes that day!

[MSG 1] range=3-8 header=None
And Saul just went wild, devastating the church, entering house after house after house, dragging men and women off to jail. Forced to leave home base, the followers of Jesus all became missionaries. Wherever they were scattered, they preached the Message about Jesus. Going down to a Samaritan city, Philip proclaimed the Message of the Messiah. When the people heard what he had to say and saw the miracles, the clear signs of God's action, they hung on his every word. Many who could neither stand nor walk were healed that day. The evil spirits protested loudly as they were sent on their way. And what joy in the city!

[MSG 2] range=9-11 header=None
Previous to Philip's arrival, a certain Simon had practiced magic in the city, posing as a famous man and dazzling all the Samaritans with his wizardry. He had them all, from little children to old men, eating out of his hand. They all thought he had supernatural powers, and called him "the Great Wizard." He had been around a long time and everyone was more or less in awe of him.

[MSG 3] range=12-13 header=None
But when Philip came to town announcing the news of God's kingdom and proclaiming the name of Jesus Christ, they forgot Simon and were baptized, becoming believers right and left! Even Simon himself believed and was baptized. From that moment he was like Philip's shadow, so fascinated with all the God-signs and miracles that he wouldn't leave Philip's side.

[MSG 4] range=14-17 header=None
When the apostles in Jerusalem received the report that Samaria had accepted God's Message, they sent Peter and John down to pray for them to receive the Holy Spirit. Up to this point they had only been baptized in the name of the Master Jesus; the Holy Spirit hadn't yet fallen on them. Then the apostles laid their hands on them and they did receive the Holy Spirit.

[MSG 5] range=18-19 header=None
When Simon saw that the apostles by merely laying on hands conferred the Spirit, he pulled out his money, excited, and said, "Sell me your secret! Show me how you did that! How much do you want? Name your price!"

[MSG 6] range=20-23 header=None
Peter said, "To hell with your money! And you along with it. Why, that's unthinkable—trying to buy God's gift! You'll never be part of what God is doing by striking bargains and offering bribes. Change your ways—and now! Ask the Master to forgive you for trying to use God to make money. I can see this is an old habit with you; you reek with money-lust."

[MSG 7] range=24 header=None
"Oh!" said Simon, "pray for me! Pray to the Master that nothing like that will ever happen to me!"

[MSG 8] range=25 header=None
And with that, the apostles were on their way, continuing to witness and spread the Message of God's salvation, preaching in every Samaritan town they passed through on their return to Jerusalem.

[MSG 9] range=26-28 header='The Ethiopian Eunuch'
Later God's angel spoke to Philip: "At noon today I want you to walk over to that desolate road that goes from Jerusalem down to Gaza." He got up and went. He met an Ethiopian eunuch coming down the road. The eunuch had been on a pilgrimage to Jerusalem and was returning to Ethiopia, where he was minister in charge of all the finances of Candace, queen of the Ethiopians. He was riding in a chariot and reading the prophet Isaiah.

[MSG 10] range=29-30 header=None
The Spirit told Philip, "Climb into the chariot." Running up alongside, Philip heard the eunuch reading Isaiah and asked, "Do you understand what you're reading?"

[MSG 11] range=31-33 header=None
He answered, "How can I without some help?" and invited Philip into the chariot with him. The passage he was reading was this: As a sheep led to slaughter,      and quiet as a lamb being sheared,  He was silent, saying nothing.      He was mocked and put down, never got a fair trial. But who now can count his kin      since he's been taken from the earth?

[MSG 12] range=34-35 header=None
The eunuch said, "Tell me, who is the prophet talking about: himself or some other?" Philip grabbed his chance. Using this passage as his text, he preached Jesus to him.

[MSG 13] range=36-39 header=None
As they continued down the road, they came to a stream of water. The eunuch said, "Here's water. Why can't I be baptized?" He ordered the chariot to stop. They both went down to the water, and Philip baptized him on the spot. When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of God suddenly took Philip off, and that was the last the eunuch saw of him. But he didn't mind. He had what he'd come for and went on down the road as happy as he could be.

[MSG 14] range=40 header=None
Philip showed up in Azotus and continued north, preaching the Message in all the villages along that route until he arrived at Caesarea.

§The Church Scatters

[EN 1] badge=1-3
So, after Stephen's crazy death, things got real for the church in Jerusalem. A man named Saul basically went on a rampage against the church, going house to house and dragging Jesus's followers to jail. It was a total mess. Because of the heat, all the believers, except for the main apostles, scattered and left town.

[EN 2] badge=4-8
But here's the cool part: as they scattered, they started spreading the word about Jesus everywhere they went. They were like secret agents for God. One of the guys, Philip, went down to a city in Samaria. He started telling everyone about Jesus, and people were hooked. He was doing all these miracles, healing people who couldn't walk, and casting out demons who were screaming as they left. The whole city was buzzing with excitement.

[EN 3] badge=9-12
Now, before Philip got there, this city had a local celebrity: a magician named Simon. This guy had everyone fooled. He did all these flashy tricks and people thought he had some kind of superpower. They called him "The Great One." But when Philip showed up with the real power of God, Simon suddenly looked like a cheap knock-off. Everyone, including Simon, believed in Jesus and got baptized. Simon was so blown away that he started following Philip everywhere, like a superfan, amazed by the real miracles.

[EN 4] badge=14-16
Back in Jerusalem, the head apostles heard that Samaria was getting into Jesus, so they sent Peter and John to check it out. When they got there, they realized the new believers had been baptized, but they hadn't received the Holy Spirit yet. So, Peter and John laid their hands on them, and BAM, they received the Holy Spirit. It was like a spiritual transformation.

[EN 5] badge=17-19
Simon the magician saw this and his jaw dropped. He saw this incredible power being given just by a touch and immediately wanted in. He pulled out his wallet and said to Peter and John, "Hey, how much for that power? I'll pay whatever you want. Just show me the trick!"

[EN 6] badge=None
§Simon's Big Mistake

[EN 7] badge=20-23
Peter was not having it. He totally roasted Simon. "To heck with you and your money! You think you can buy a gift from God? You are so out of line. You're all twisted up inside with greed. You better pray to God and hope he forgives you for thinking something so messed up." Simon got super scared and begged them, "Oh man, please pray for me! Pray that I don't get wrecked for this!"

[EN 8] badge=25
After that, Peter and John headed back to Jerusalem, but they preached in a bunch of other Samaritan towns on the way.

[EN 9] badge=None
§Philip and the Ethiopian

[EN 10] badge=26-28
Then, an angel of God gave Philip a new mission. "Hey, go south to that desert road that goes from Jerusalem to Gaza." So Philip just went. On the road, he saw a super important guy from Ethiopia. This guy was the treasurer for the queen of Ethiopia, basically the important official in charge of all the money. He was riding in his fancy chariot, reading the book of Isaiah.

[EN 11] badge=29-30
The Holy Spirit told Philip, "Go catch up to that chariot." Philip ran up and heard the guy reading. He asked him, "Hey, do you get what you're reading?"

[EN 12] badge=31-33
The Ethiopian guy was like, "How can I? I need someone to explain it to me." He invited Philip to hop in the chariot with him. The part he was reading was about a sheep being led to be slaughtered, who didn't make a sound. It was about someone who was treated unfairly and then taken from the earth.

[EN 13] badge=34-35
"So," the Ethiopian asked, "is the prophet talking about himself, or someone else?" This was the perfect opening. Philip started with that exact verse and told him all about Jesus.

[EN 14] badge=36-39
As they were riding, they passed some water. The Ethiopian said, "Look, water! What's stopping me from getting baptized right now?" He ordered the chariot to stop, and they both went down into the water, and Philip baptized him. When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of God suddenly teleported Philip away. The Ethiopian never saw him again, but he didn't even care. He was so happy because he had found what he was looking for. He went on his way, totally joyful.

[EN 15] badge=40
Philip reappeared in another town called Azotus and kept on traveling, telling everyone the good news about Jesus in all the towns until he got to Caesarea.
