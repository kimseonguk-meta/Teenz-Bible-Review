### TITLE KO: 새로운 미션

[MSG 0] range=1-5 header='To the Ends of the World'
Dear Theophilus, in the first volume of this book I wrote on everything that Jesus began to do and teach until the day he said good-bye to the apostles, the ones he had chosen through the Holy Spirit, and was taken up to heaven. After his death, he presented himself alive to them in many different settings over a period of forty days. In face-to-face meetings, he talked to them about things concerning the kingdom of God. As they met and ate meals together, he told them that they were on no account to leave Jerusalem but "must wait for what the Father promised: the promise you heard from me. John baptized in water; you will be baptized in the Holy Spirit. And soon."

[MSG 1] range=6 header=None
When they were together for the last time they asked, "Master, are you going to restore the kingdom to Israel now? Is this the time?"

[MSG 2] range=7-8 header=None
He told them, "You don't get to know the time. Timing is the Father's business. What you'll get is the Holy Spirit. And when the Holy Spirit comes on you, you will be able to be my witnesses in Jerusalem, all over Judea and Samaria, even to the ends of the world."

[MSG 3] range=9-11 header=None
These were his last words. As they watched, he was taken up and disappeared in a cloud. They stood there, staring into the empty sky. Suddenly two men appeared—in white robes! They said, "You Galileans!—why do you just stand here looking up at an empty sky? This very Jesus who was taken up from among you to heaven will come as certainly—and mysteriously—as he left."

[MSG 4] range=12-13 header='Returning to Jerusalem'
So they left the mountain called Olives and returned to Jerusalem. It was a little over half a mile. They went to the upper room they had been using as a meeting place: Peter, John,, James,, Andrew,, Philip,, Thomas,, Bartholomew,, Matthew, James, son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, Judas, son of James.

[MSG 5] range=14 header=None
They agreed they were in this for good, completely together in prayer, the women included. Also Jesus' mother, Mary, and his brothers.

[MSG 6] range=15-17 header='Replacing Judas'
During this time, Peter stood up in the company—there were about 120 of them in the room at the time—and said, "Friends, long ago the Holy Spirit spoke through David regarding Judas, who became the guide to those who arrested Jesus. That Scripture had to be fulfilled, and now has been. Judas was one of us and had his assigned place in this ministry.

[MSG 7] range=18-20 header=None
"As you know, he took the evil bribe money and bought a small farm. There he came to a bad end, rupturing his belly and spilling his guts. Everybody in Jerusalem knows this by now; they call the place Murder Meadow. It's exactly what we find written in the Psalms: Let his farm become haunted  So no one can ever live there. "And also what was written later: Let someone else take over his post.

[MSG 8] range=21-22 header=None
"Judas must now be replaced. The replacement must come from the company of men who stayed together with us from the time Jesus was baptized by John up to the day of his ascension, designated along with us as a witness to his resurrection."

[MSG 9] range=23-26 header=None
They nominated two: Joseph Barsabbas, nicknamed Justus, and Matthias. Then they prayed, "You, O God, know every one of us inside and out. Make plain which of these two men you choose to take the place in this ministry and leadership that Judas threw away in order to go his own way." They then drew straws. Matthias won and was counted in with the eleven apostles.

§Jesus Goes Up

[EN 1] badge=1-3
Yo Theophilus, what's up? In my first book, I told you all about what Jesus did and taught, right up until he peaced out and went to heaven. After he came back from the dead, he hung out with his chosen apostles for 40 days, proving he was for real. They just chilled, and he told them all about God's kingdom.

[EN 2] badge=4-5
One time, while they were eating, he gave them a direct order: "Don't leave Jerusalem. Wait for the special gift my Father promised, the one I told you about. John the Baptist just used water, but you guys are about to get a major upgrade with the Holy Spirit, and it's coming soon."

[EN 3] badge=6-7
So, when they were all together for the last time, they asked him, "Hey, boss, is it time? Are you gonna make Israel the top dog again now?" Jesus was like, "Chill, the timing is my Father's business, not yours. But here's what you *do* get: the Holy Spirit. When he comes, you'll get this insane power to be my witnesses. You'll start in Jerusalem, then go all over Judea and Samaria, and then, like, to the whole world."

[EN 4] badge=8-9
Right after he said that, he started floating up into the sky and disappeared into a cloud. They were all just standing there, staring up, totally stunned. Suddenly, two guys in bright white clothes appeared next to them and said, "You guys from Galilee, why are you just staring at the sky? This same Jesus, who was just taken from you into heaven, is going to come back in the exact same way you saw him go."

[EN 5] badge=10-13
After that, the crew walked back to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives, which was like a short walk. They went straight to the upstairs room where they were staying. The whole squad was there: Peter, John, James, Andrew, Philip, Thomas, Bartholomew, Matthew, James (the son of Alphaeus), Simon the Zealot, and Judas (the son of James).

[EN 6] badge=14
They were all on the same page, spending all their time praying together. The women were there too, along with Jesus's mom, Mary, and his brothers. They were all in.

[EN 7] badge=15
After a few days, Peter stood up in front of the whole group, about 120 people, and said, "Listen up, everyone. The prophecy from the Holy Spirit, spoken by King David, had to come true. It was about Judas, the guy who betrayed Jesus and led the temple guards to arrest him. He was one of us, part of the team.

[EN 8] badge=16-18
(Here's the dirt on Judas: he took the bribe money and bought a field. But then he had a gnarly fall, his stomach burst open, and all his guts spilled out. Everyone in Jerusalem heard about it, and they even named the field 'Field of Blood.')

[EN 9] badge=19-20
"It's written in the book of Psalms: 'Let his place be empty; let no one live there,' and also, 'Let someone else take his job.' So now we have to pick a replacement for Judas. It has to be one of the guys who was with us the whole time, from when Jesus was baptized by John until the day he was taken up to heaven. This new guy will be a witness with us that Jesus rose from the dead."

[EN 10] badge=21-26
They nominated two guys: Joseph, who was also called Barsabbas (and nicknamed Justus), and Matthias. Then they prayed, "God, you know everyone's heart. Show us which of these two you've chosen to take over this ministry and leadership role that Judas ditched to go to his own place." Then they drew straws, and the lot fell to Matthias. So he was officially added to the eleven apostles, making them a solid twelve again.
