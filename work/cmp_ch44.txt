===== CHAPTER 44: A Brother's Desperate Plea to Save Another (20 EN, 20 KO, 18 MSG) =====
[EN0 1-2] Joseph told his head servant, “Load up their bags with as much food as they can hold, and stick their money back in the top of each bag. And in the youngest one’s bag, put my personal silver cup, right on top of his money.” The servant did exactly what Joseph said.
[EN1 None] § The Chase Is On
[EN2 3-5] As soon as the sun was up, the brothers and their donkeys were on their way. They hadn’t even gotten out of the city when Joseph told his steward, “Go after them! When you catch them, say, ‘Why would you repay good with evil? That cup you took is the one my master drinks from and even uses to figure stuff out. What you did is seriously messed up!’”
[EN3 6-10] The steward caught up and said exactly that. The brothers were like, “Whoa, what are you talking about? We’d never do that! Look, we brought back the money we found in our bags all the way from Canaan. You really think we’d steal from your master now? If you find that cup on any of us, that person can die, and the rest of us will be your master’s slaves.”
[EN4 9] The steward replied, “Okay, fine, but let’s not get that extreme. Whoever has the cup will be my slave, but the rest of you are free to go.”
[EN5 11-12] They all rushed to get their bags on the ground and opened them up. The steward searched everything, starting with the oldest and ending with the youngest. And boom—the cup was in Benjamin’s bag.
[EN6 13] They completely lost it, tearing their clothes in frustration. They loaded up their donkeys and headed back to the city.
[EN7 None] § The Confrontation
[EN8 14] Joseph was still at his house when Judah and his brothers got there. They immediately threw themselves on the ground in front of him.
[EN9 15] Joseph hit them with, “How could you do this? You had to know a guy like me would figure this out.”
[EN10 16] Judah, speaking for everyone, said, “What can we even say, master? There’s nothing to say. How can we prove we’re innocent? God is the one behind this, showing us how messed up we are. We’re guilty. We’ll be your slaves—all of us, not just the one with the cup.”
[EN11 17] “No way, I’d never do that,” Joseph said. “Only the guy who had the cup will be my slave. The rest of you can go back to your father in peace.”
[EN12 None] § Judah's Plea
[EN13 18-20] Judah stepped up and said, “Please, master, can I just say one thing? Please don’t get mad. To me, you’re just as powerful as Pharaoh. You asked us, ‘Do you have a father or a brother?’ And we told you the truth, ‘We have an old father, and a younger brother who was born when he was old. His other brother is dead, so he’s the only one left from his mom, and his dad loves him like crazy.’
[EN14 21-22] “Then you told us, ‘Bring him here so I can see him.’ We told you it was impossible, that the boy couldn’t leave his father because his father would die if he did.
[EN15 23] “But then you said, ‘If your youngest brother doesn’t come with you, you’ll never see my face again.’
[EN16 24-26] “When we got back to our father, we told him everything you said. So when our dad told us to go buy more food, we had to tell him straight up, ‘We can’t. The only way we can go is if our youngest brother comes with us. We’re not even allowed to see the man unless our youngest brother is there.’
[EN17 27-29] “Our father, your servant, told us, ‘You know my wife gave me two sons. One of them disappeared, and I’m pretty sure he was torn apart by animals. I’ve never seen him since. If you take this one from me too and something bad happens to him, you’ll be sending my grieving, old self to the grave.’
[EN18 30-31] “So, you see, if I go back to my father without the boy—his life is totally wrapped up in this kid’s life—the second he sees the boy isn’t with us, he’ll die. He’ll literally die of grief, and we’ll be the ones who killed him. Plus, I personally guaranteed his safety to my father. I promised him, ‘If I don’t bring him back, I’ll take the blame for the rest of my life.’
[EN19 32-34] “So please, let me stay here as your slave instead of the boy. Let the boy go back with his brothers. How can I possibly go back to my father if the boy isn’t with me? I can’t stand to watch my father die of a broken heart!”
