===== CHAPTER 40: Dreams, Drama, and a Forgotten Promise (13 EN, 13 KO, 9 MSG) =====
[EN0 1-4] So, after a while, the king of Egypt's personal drink server and his main baker messed up and made their boss, the king, super mad. Pharaoh was so ticked off at them that he threw them in the same jail where Joseph was, under the watch of the captain of the guard. The captain put Joseph in charge of them.
[EN1 None] § Bummer Dreams in Jail
[EN2 5-7] After they'd been locked up for a bit, both the cupbearer and the baker had these intense dreams on the same night, and each dream felt like it meant something specific. When Joseph came to check on them in the morning, he saw they were totally bummed out. He asked them, 'What's up? Why the long faces?'
[EN3 8] They were like, 'We both had these crazy dreams, but there's no one to tell us what they mean.' Joseph replied, 'Doesn't God know what dreams mean? Just tell me what you dreamed.'
[EN4 None] § The Cupbearer's Dream
[EN5 9-11] So the head cupbearer went first. 'Okay, so in my dream, there was this vine right in front of me with three branches. It grew buds, then flowers, and then boom—ripe grapes. I was holding Pharaoh's cup, so I grabbed the grapes, squeezed the juice into his cup, and handed it to him.'
[EN6 12-15] Joseph was like, 'Alright, here's the deal. The three branches are three days. In three days, Pharaoh's gonna let you out and give you your old job back. You'll be handing him his cup just like you used to. Just, when things are good for you again, please remember me. Tell Pharaoh about me and get me out of this place. I was literally kidnapped from my home, and I've done nothing to deserve being stuck in this hole.'
[EN7 None] § The Baker's Dream
[EN8 16-17] When the head baker saw how well that went, he was like, 'My turn! My dream was like this: I had three big baskets stacked on my head. The top one was full of all kinds of pastries for the king, but birds were flying down and eating them right out of the basket.'
[EN9 18-19] Joseph’s face fell. 'Man, this is what it means: The three baskets are also three days. In three days, Pharaoh is going to chop off your head, stick you on a pole, and birds will eat your body. It's grim, I know.'
[EN10 None] § A Forgotten Promise
[EN11 20-22] And for real, three days later it was Pharaoh's birthday, and he threw a huge party for his whole staff. He brought out the cupbearer and the baker and put them in front of everyone. He gave the cupbearer his old job back, and he was serving Pharaoh his wine again. But then, he had the baker executed and impaled on a post, exactly like Joseph said would happen.
[EN12 23] But the head cupbearer? He totally spaced on Joseph. He forgot all about him.
----- MSG -----
[MSG0 40] 1-4 As time went on, it happened that the cupbearer and the baker of the king of Egypt crossed their master, the king of Egypt. Pharaoh was furious with his two officials, the head cupbearer and the head baker, and put them in custody under the captain of the guard; it was the same jail where Joseph was held. The captain of the guard assigned Joseph to see to their needs.
[MSG1 4-7] After they had been in custody for a while, the king’s cupbearer and baker, while being held in the jail, both had a dream on the same night, each dream having its own meaning. When Joseph arrived in the morning, he noticed that they were feeling low. So he asked them, the two officials of Pharaoh who had been thrown into jail with him, “What’s wrong? Why the long faces?”
[MSG2 8] They said, “We dreamed dreams and there’s no one to interpret them.” Joseph said, “Don’t interpretations come from God? Tell me the dreams.”
[MSG3 9-11] First the head cupbearer told his dream to Joseph: “In my dream there was a vine in front of me with three branches on it: It budded, blossomed, and the clusters ripened into grapes. I was holding Pharaoh’s cup; I took the grapes, squeezed them into Pharaoh’s cup, and gave the cup to Pharaoh.”
[MSG4 12-15] Joseph said, “Here’s the meaning. The three branches are three days. Within three days, Pharaoh will get you out of here and put you back to your old work—you’ll be giving Pharaoh his cup just as you used to do when you were his cupbearer. Only remember me when things are going well with you again—tell Pharaoh about me and get me out of this place. I was kidnapped from the land of the Hebrews. And since I’ve been here, I’ve done nothing to deserve being put in this hole.”
[MSG5 16-17] When the head baker saw how well Joseph’s interpretation turned out, he spoke up: “My dream went like this: I saw three wicker baskets on my head; the top basket had assorted pastries from the bakery and birds were picking at them from the basket on my head.”
[MSG6 18-19] Joseph said, “This is the interpretation: The three baskets are three days; within three days Pharaoh will take off your head, impale you on a post, and the birds will pick your bones clean.”
[MSG7 20-22] And sure enough, on the third day it was Pharaoh’s birthday and he threw a feast for all his servants. He set the head cupbearer and the head baker in places of honor in the presence of all the guests. Then he restored the head cupbearer to his cupbearing post; he handed Pharaoh his cup just as before. And then he impaled the head baker on a post, following Joseph’s interpretations exactly.
[MSG8 23] But the head cupbearer gave Joseph another thought; he forgot all about him.
