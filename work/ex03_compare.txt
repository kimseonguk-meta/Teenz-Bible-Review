--- MSG [1-2] ---
Moses was shepherding the flock of Jethro, his father-in-law, the priest of Midian. He led the flock to the west end of the wilderness and came to the mountain of God, Horeb. The angel of God appeared to him in flames of fire blazing out of the middle of a bush. He looked. The bush was blazing away but it didn’t burn up.

>>> TEEN:
  [p0 badge=1-2] So, Moses was looking after the sheep for his father-in-law, Jethro, who was the priest of Midian. He led the flock way out to the west side of the desert and ended up at Horeb, the mountain of God. Out of nowhere, an angel of God appeared to him in a fire blazing from the middle of a bush. Moses looked, and for real, the bush was on fire but wasn't burning up.
  [KO p0] 모세가 미디안의 제사장이자 자기 장인인 이드로의 양 떼를 치고 있었대. 양 떼를 이끌고 광야 서쪽 끝으로 갔는데, 거기서 '호렙'이라는 하나님의 산에 도착한 거임. 근데 갑자기 떨기나무 한가운데서 불꽃이 막 타오르더니, 거기서 하나님의 천사가 나타난 거야. 모세가 딱 봤는데, 헐, 나무가 불타고 있는데 안 없어지는 거임.
