[EN5 10-11] Jacob dipped from Beersheba and headed to Haran. He found a random spot to crash for the night 'cause the sun was down. He grabbed a rock for a pillow (for real) and went to sleep. And then he had this epic dream: there was a massive ladder set up on the ground, and it went all the way up to the sky. And get this—angels were going up and down on it.
[EN6 12-15] Then, boom, God was right there in front of him, saying, "I'm God, the God of your grandpa Abraham and your dad Isaac. This ground you're sleeping on? I'm giving it to you and your kids. Your family's gonna be like the dust of the earth—they're gonna spread out in every direction. All the people on earth will be blessed because of you and your fam. For real. [EN7 16-17] Jacob woke up and was like, "Whoa, God is actually in this place, and I had no idea!" He was totally freaked out. He whispered, "This is insane. So cool. So holy. This is God's house. This is the gate to heaven."
[EN8 18-19] First thing in the morning, Jacob got up, took that rock he used as a pillow, and set it up as a memorial. He poured oil on it to make it official. He named the place Bethel, which means "God's House." Before that, the town was called Luz.
[EN9 20-22] Then Jacob made a promise, like a serious vow: "If God sticks with me and keeps me safe on this trip, gives me food and clothes, and gets me back to my dad's house in one piece, then this God will be my God. And this rock I set up? It's gonna be a sign that this is a place where God lives. And out of everything you give me, I'll give a tenth of it back to you."
