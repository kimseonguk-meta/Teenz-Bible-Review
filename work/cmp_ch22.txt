===== CHAPTER 22: A Test of Faith: Abraham and Isaac (20 EN, 20 KO, 15 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § The Ultimate Test
[EN1 1] So after all this went down, God decided to test Abraham’s loyalty. He called out, “Abraham!” and Abraham replied, “Yeah? What’s up?”
[EN2 2] God said, “Take your son, your only son Isaac, the one you love so much, and go to the region of Moriah. There, you’re to sacrifice him as a burnt offering on one of the mountains I’ll show you.”
[EN3 3-5] Abraham, being the dedicated guy he was, got up super early the next morning. He saddled his donkey, and took two of his servants and his son Isaac with him. He had already chopped the wood for the offering, and they set off for the place God told him about. On the third day, Abraham looked up and saw the spot from a distance. He told his servants, “Yo, just wait here with the donkey. The boy and I are going over there to worship, and we’ll be right back.”
[EN4 None] § A Father and Son's Journey
[EN5 6] Abraham handed the wood for the burnt offering to Isaac to carry, while he carried the flint and the knife. As they were walking, Isaac turned to his dad and said, “Hey, Dad?”
[EN6 7] “Yeah, son?”
[EN7 7] “So, we’ve got the flint and the wood… but where’s the lamb for the offering?”
[EN8 8] Abraham answered, “Don’t worry, son. God will provide the lamb.” And they continued their journey together.
[EN9 None] § The Altar
[EN10 9-10] When they got to the place God had directed them to, Abraham built an altar and arranged the wood on it. Then, he tied up his son Isaac and laid him on the altar, on top of the wood. He reached for the knife, about to sacrifice his son.
[EN11 11] But just then, an angel of God called out to him from heaven, “Abraham! Abraham!”
[EN12 11] “I’m here!” he answered.
[EN13 12] “Don’t you dare lay a hand on that boy!” the angel said. “Don’t hurt him in any way. Now I know that you truly fear God, because you were willing to sacrifice your own son, your only son, for me.”
[EN14 13-14] Abraham looked up and saw a ram caught by its horns in a thicket. He went and got the ram, and sacrificed it as a burnt offering instead of his son. Abraham named that place “Yahweh-Yireh,” which means “The Lord Will Provide.” And to this day, people say, “On the mountain of the Lord it will be provided.”
[EN15 None] § A Promise Reconfirmed
[EN16 15-18] The angel of God called out to Abraham from heaven a second time, saying, “I swear by myself, declares the Lord, that because you have done this and have not withheld your son, your only son, I will surely bless you and make your descendants as numerous as the stars in the sky and as the sand on the seashore. Your descendants will take possession of the cities of their enemies, and through your offspring all nations on earth will be blessed, because you have obeyed me.”
[EN17 19] Then Abraham returned to his servants, and they all went back to Beersheba, where Abraham settled down.
[EN18 None] § Family Update
[EN19 20-24] After all this, Abraham got some news about his brother Nahor. Turns out, his wife Milcah had given him a bunch of kids: Uz the firstborn, his brother Buz, Kemuel (the father of Aram), Kesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel. And Bethuel was the father of Rebekah. These were the eight sons Milcah had with Nahor, Abraham’s brother. Nahor also had four other children with his concubine, Reumah: Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah.
