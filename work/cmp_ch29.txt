===== CHAPTER 29: Jacob's Crazy Love Story: 14 Years for Rachel (30 EN, 30 KO, 17 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § Jacob Meets Rachel
[EN1 1-3] So Jacob bounced and headed east. He saw a well in a field with three groups of sheep chilling around it. This well was where everyone got water for their flocks. A massive stone covered the well's opening. The shepherds would only roll the stone away to water the sheep when all the flocks were there, and then they'd put it back.
[EN2 4] Jacob walked up and said, "'Sup, guys. Where are you from?"
[EN3 4] They replied, "We're from Haran."
[EN4 5] Jacob asked, "You know Laban, Nahor's son?"
[EN5 5] “Yeah, we know him.”
[EN6 6] “Is he doing good?” Jacob asked.
[EN7 6] “Totally,” they said. “And look, here comes his daughter Rachel with the sheep.”
[EN8 7] Jacob was like, “It's still pretty early, the sun's high up. Isn't it too soon to bring the sheep in? Why not water them and let them graze some more?”
[EN9 8] “We can't,” they explained. “We have to wait for all the other shepherds. It takes all of us to move that huge stone. Only then can we water the flocks.”
[EN10 9-12] While they were talking, Rachel showed up with her dad's sheep. She was the one taking care of them. The second Jacob saw Rachel, his uncle's daughter, with his uncle's sheep, he went over and, all by himself, rolled the stone away from the well and watered his uncle Laban's flock. Then he kissed Rachel and just started crying. He told her he was her dad's relative, Rebekah's son. She immediately ran and told her father.
[EN11 13] When Laban heard the news about his sister's son, Jacob, he ran out to meet him. He gave him a big hug and a kiss and brought him home. Jacob told Laban his whole story.
[EN12 14] Laban was like, “You're for real family! My own flesh and blood!”
[EN13 None] § Jacob Gets Tricked
[EN14 14-15] After Jacob had stayed with him for a month, Laban said, “Look, just because you're my nephew doesn't mean you should work for free. Tell me what you want. What's a fair wage?”
[EN15 16-17] Now, Laban had two daughters. The older one was Leah, and the younger one was Rachel. Leah had nice eyes, but Rachel was a total knockout, like, drop-dead gorgeous. And Jacob was completely in love with Rachel.
[EN16 18] So Jacob said, “I'll work for you for seven years if I can marry your younger daughter, Rachel.”
[EN17 19] Laban replied, “It's way better for me to give her to you than to some random guy. For sure. Stay here with me.”
[EN18 20] So Jacob worked for seven years for Rachel. But because he was so in love with her, it felt like only a few days.
[EN19 21] Then Jacob went to Laban and said, “I've done my part. Give me my wife. I'm ready to get married.”
[EN20 22-24] So Laban threw a huge party and invited everyone from the area. But when evening came, he brought his daughter Leah to Jacob, and Jacob slept with her. (Laban also gave his servant girl Zilpah to Leah.)
[EN21 25] When morning came, Jacob saw it was Leah in his bed!
[EN22 25] He went straight to Laban and was like, “What did you do to me?! Didn't I work all this time for Rachel? Why did you trick me?”
[EN23 26] Laban answered, “That's not how we do things here. We don't marry off the younger daughter before the older one. Finish the week-long wedding celebration with Leah, and then we'll give you Rachel too. But you'll have to work for me for another seven years.”
[EN24 27-30] Jacob agreed. After the week was over, Laban gave him his daughter Rachel as his wife. (Laban also gave his servant girl Bilhah to Rachel.) So Jacob slept with Rachel, and he loved her way more than Leah. And he worked for Laban for another seven years.
[EN25 None] § The Baby Competition
[EN26 31-32] When God saw that Leah wasn't loved, he made it so she could have kids, but Rachel couldn't. Leah got pregnant and had a son. She named him Reuben, which means “Look, a son!” She said, “This is a sign that God has seen how miserable I am, and now my husband will finally love me.”
[EN27 33] She got pregnant again and had another son. She said, “God heard that I was unloved, so he gave me this son too.” She named him Simeon, which means “God heard.”
[EN28 34] She got pregnant again and had another son. She said, “Now, for sure, my husband will get attached to me. I've given him three sons!” That's why he was named Levi, which means “attached.”
[EN29 35] She got pregnant one last time and had a fourth son. She said, “This time, I'm just going to praise God.” So she named him Judah, which means “praise.” After that, she stopped having kids.
