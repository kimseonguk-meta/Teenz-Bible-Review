[EN4 None] § Isaac's Risky Lie
[EN5 7] The local guys started asking him about his wife. He told them, “She’s my sister.” He was too scared to say, “She’s my wife,” because he was thinking, “These dudes might kill me to get Rebekah, she’s seriously beautiful.”
[EN6 8-10] After they had been there for a good while, King Abimelech looked out his window one day and saw Isaac getting a little too cozy with his wife, Rebekah. Busted. Abimelech called Isaac over and said, “Okay, so she IS your wife. Why on earth did you tell us she was your sister?”
[EN7 9] Isaac replied, “Because I honestly thought I might get killed by someone who wanted her.”
[EN8 10-11] Abimelech was like, “Dude, what were you thinking? One of the men could have easily slept with your wife, and you would have brought a huge mess down on all of us!” Then Abimelech made a decree to everyone: “Anyone who even lays a hand on this man or his wife will be executed. Period.”
[EN9 None] § Well Drama and Jealous Haters
[EN10 12-14] Isaac planted crops in that land and had an insane harvest. God totally blessed him. The guy got richer and richer every single day until he was legit wealthy. He had tons of flocks, herds, and a whole army of servants. It got to the point where the Philistines were super jealous.
[EN11 15] They were so petty that they went and filled up all the wells that his father’s servants had dug back in Abraham's time with dirt, completely clogging them.
[EN12 16] Finally, Abimelech told Isaac, “You need to leave. You’ve become way too powerful for us.”
[EN13 17-18] So Isaac left and set up camp in the Gerar valley. He started re-digging the wells that were from his father Abraham’s time, which the Philistines had plugged up after Abraham died. He even gave them the same names his father had originally given them.
[EN14 19-21] One day, while Isaac’s servants were digging, they found a well with fresh spring water. But the shepherds from Gerar started a fight with Isaac’s shepherds, yelling, “This water is ours!” So Isaac named the well Esek, which means “Quarrel,” because they fought over it. Then they dug another well, and people argued over that one too, so he named it Sitnah, meaning “Accusation.”
[EN15 22] He moved on from there and dug another well. This time, nobody fought over it, so he named it Rehoboth, which means “Wide-Open Spaces.” He said, “Finally! God has given us room to grow and succeed in this land.”
[EN16 23-24] From there, he went up to Beersheba. That very night, God appeared to him and said, “I am the God of your father, Abraham. Don’t be afraid, because I am with you. I will bless you and make your family huge, all for the sake of my servant Abraham.”
[EN17 25] Isaac built an altar there and prayed to God. He set up his tent, and his servants started digging yet another well.
[EN18 None] § Making Peace
[EN19 26-27] Then Abimelech came to visit him from Gerar, bringing his advisor Ahuzzath and Phicol, the commander of his army. Isaac asked them, “Why are you guys here? You obviously hate me; you kicked me out of your country.”
[EN20 28-29] They answered, “Look, we’ve seen that God is totally on your side. We want to make a peace treaty with you, a promise that we’ll stay on good terms. We never bothered you; in fact, we were good to you and let you leave in peace. So, for real, God’s blessing is with you!”
[EN21 30-31] Isaac threw a big feast for them, and they all ate and drank together. They got up early the next morning and made a formal oath to each other. Then Isaac said his goodbyes, and they left as friends.
[EN22 32-33] Later that same day, Isaac’s servants came to him with awesome news about the well they were digging: “We hit water!” Isaac named the well Sheba, which means “Oath.” And that’s why the city is called Beersheba, or “Oath-Well,” to this day.
[EN23 None] § Esau's Bad Decisions
[EN24 34-35] When Esau was forty, he married two Hittite women, Judith and Basemath. They made life a living nightmare for Isaac and Rebekah.
