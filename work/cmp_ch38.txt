===== CHAPTER 38: A Wild Family Scandal and a Clever Plan (27 EN, 27 KO, 18 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § Judah's Family Drama
[EN1 1-5] So, around that time, Judah decided to leave his brothers and go hang out with this guy from Adullam named Hirah. While he was there, he met a Canaanite girl, the daughter of a man named Shua. He married her, they hooked up, and she had a son named Er. Then she got pregnant again and had another son, Onan. After that, she had one more son and named him Shelah. They were living in a place called Kezib when he was born.
[EN2 6-7] Judah found a wife for his first son, Er. Her name was Tamar. But Er, Judah's oldest, did something that really ticked God off, so God ended his life. Not cool.
[EN3 None] § Onan's Selfish Move
[EN4 8-10] So Judah told his second son, Onan, "Dude, you gotta sleep with your brother's widow. It's your duty to make sure your brother has kids to carry on his name." But Onan knew the kid wouldn't technically be his, so every time he slept with his brother's widow, he... well, he made sure not to get her pregnant. God was super offended by this and took his life, too. Seriously, not a good look.
[EN5 11] Judah, probably freaking out a little, told his daughter-in-law Tamar, "Hey, just go back to your dad's house and live as a widow until my youngest son, Shelah, grows up." He was secretly worried Shelah would end up dead like his brothers. So Tamar went back home.
[EN6 None] § Tamar's Crazy Plan
[EN7 12] Time went by, and Judah's wife, Shua's daughter, died. After the mourning period was over, Judah and his buddy Hirah went to a town called Timnah for the sheep shearing festival.
[EN8 13-14] Someone told Tamar, "Yo, your father-in-law is heading to Timnah to shear his sheep." This was her chance. She took off her widow's clothes, put on a veil so no one would recognize her, and sat by the entrance to Enaim, which was on the way to Timnah. She had figured out that even though Shelah was all grown up, she was never going to get to marry him.
[EN9 15] Judah saw her sitting there and, because of the veil, thought she was an immoral woman. He walked over to her and said, "Hey, let's do this." He had zero clue it was his own daughter-in-law.
[EN10 16] She played it cool and asked, "What's in it for me?"
[EN11 17] He said, "I'll send you a young goat from my flock."
[EN12 17] She replied, "Okay, but you have to give me something as a promise until you send it."
[EN13 18] "What do you want as a promise?" he asked.
[EN14 18] She said, "Your seal, your cord, and that staff you're carrying." He handed them over, they slept together, and she got pregnant.
[EN15 19] Afterward, she went home, took off the veil, and put her widow's clothes back on.
[EN16 None] § The Big Reveal
[EN17 20-22] Judah sent the goat with his friend Hirah to get his stuff back from the woman, but Hirah couldn't find her. He asked the local guys, "Where's the immoral woman that usually hangs out by the road near Enaim?"
[EN18 22] They were like, "Uh, there's never been an immoral woman here, dude."
[EN19 23] So Hirah went back to Judah and said, "I couldn't find her. The locals said there's never been an immoral woman there."
[EN20 23] Judah was like, "Whatever, just let her keep the stuff. If we keep looking, we'll just become a joke. I sent the goat, but you couldn't find her. My part's done."
[EN21 24] About three months later, someone told Judah, "Your daughter-in-law Tamar has been acting like a total immoral woman, and now she's pregnant."
[EN22 24] Judah was furious and yelled, "Get her out here! We're gonna burn her!"
[EN23 25] But as they were dragging her out, she sent a message to her father-in-law. It said, "I'm pregnant by the man who owns these things. Take a good look. Who owns this seal, this cord, and this staff?"
[EN24 26] Judah recognized them immediately. He said, "She's right, and I'm wrong. I'm the one who didn't let her marry my son Shelah." And he never slept with her again.
[EN25 None] § A Tale of Two Twins
[EN26 27-30] When it was time for her to give birth, surprise! She was having twins. As she was in labor, one of the babies stuck his hand out. The midwife tied a red thread around his wrist and said, "This one's coming out first!" But then he pulled his hand back in, and his brother came out first. The midwife was like, "Whoa, a total breakout!" So they named him Perez, which means "Breakout." Then his brother, the one with the red thread on his hand, was born. They named him Zerah, which means "Bright."
