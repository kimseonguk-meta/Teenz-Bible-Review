[EN5 None] § Sarah's New Name and a Surprise Baby
[EN6 15-16] God wasn’t done yet. He said to Abraham, “And your wife, Sarai? Don’t call her Sarai anymore. Call her Sarah. I’m gonna bless her, for real! I’m giving you a son through her! Oh man, I’m gonna bless her so much. Nations will come from her, and kings of nations will come from her.”
[EN7 17] Abraham fell on his face again. But this time, he laughed. He was thinking, “For real? A 100-year-old dude is gonna be a dad? And Sarah, at 90, is gonna have a baby?”
[EN8 18] He pulled himself together and said to God, “Yo, just please keep Ishmael safe and sound!”
[EN9 19] But God was like, “That’s not what I’m talking about. Your wife, Sarah, is gonna have a baby boy. You’re gonna name him Isaac, which means ‘Laughter.’ I’m gonna make my forever promise with him and his descendants.

[EN11 22] Then God finished talking with Abraham and left.
[EN12 None] § Keeping the Promise
[EN13 23-27] That very same day, Abraham took his son Ishmael and all his servants, everyone from his household, and circumcised them, just like God told him to. Abraham was 99 when he was circumcised, and his son Ishmael was 13. Abraham and Ishmael got circumcised on the same day, along with all the servants in his house, no matter where they came from. Everyone got circumcised with him.
