===== CHAPTER 19: Sodom's Downfall and a Shocking Family Secret (29 EN, 30 KO, 21 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § An Awkward Welcome
[EN1 1-2] That evening, the two angels showed up in Sodom. Lot was chilling by the city gate and when he saw them, he got up, bowed down, and was like, “Hey guys, please come crash at my place for the night. You can wash up and then get an early start tomorrow, all refreshed.”
[EN2 2] They were like, “Nah, we’re good, we’ll just sleep on the street.”
[EN3 3] But Lot was super insistent and wouldn’t take no for an answer. So, they finally gave in and went home with him. Lot cooked them a hot meal, and they all ate.
[EN4 None] § A Vicious Mob
[EN5 4-5] Before they could even go to bed, a huge mob of guys from all over Sodom, young and old, surrounded the house. They were yelling at Lot, “Where are the dudes who are staying with you tonight? Bring them out so we can have sex with them!”
[EN6 6-8] Lot went outside, shutting the door behind him, and pleaded, “Guys, please, don’t be so messed up. Look, I’ve got two daughters who are virgins. I’ll bring them out and you can do whatever you want with them, but please don’t touch these men. They’re my guests.”
[EN7 9] But they just yelled back, “Get out of our way! You just show up here and now you think you can tell us what to do? We’ll do way worse to you than to them!” They rushed at Lot and tried to break down the door.
[EN8 10-11] But the two visitors reached out, pulled Lot back inside, and locked the door. Then they zapped the entire mob with blindness, so everyone from the leaders to the followers was just stumbling around in the dark, totally clueless.
[EN9 None] § Time to Bail
[EN10 12-13] The two men said to Lot, “You got any other family here? Sons, daughters, anyone at all in this city? You need to get them out of here, like, right now. We’re about to destroy this place. The cries of the victims have reached God, and He sent us to totally wipe this city off the map.”
[EN11 14] So Lot went out and warned his daughters’ fiancés, “You guys have to get out of here! God is about to destroy this city!” But they just laughed and thought he was joking.
[EN12 15] As soon as the sun started to rise, the angels were pushing Lot to get a move on. “Hurry up! Get your wife and your two daughters out of here before it’s too late and you get caught up in the city’s punishment.”
[EN13 16-17] But Lot was dragging his feet. So the men, because God was being super merciful, grabbed Lot, his wife, and his daughters by the arm and dragged them to safety outside the city. Once they were out, one of them said, “Now run for your life! Don’t look back, and don’t stop anywhere on the plain. Run to the hills, or you’ll be toast.”
[EN14 None] § A Small Request
[EN15 18-21] But Lot was like, “No way, masters, you can’t be serious! I know you guys have been cool to me and saved my life, which is a huge deal, but I can’t make it to the mountains. Something terrible will probably happen to me there and I’ll die. But look, that town over there is super close. It’s just a tiny place, barely anything. Let me escape there and live. It’s basically just a wide spot in the road.”
[EN16 21-22] “Alright, fine,” he said. “If you insist. I’ll do what you want. I won’t destroy the town you pointed out. But seriously, hurry up. Run! I can’t do anything until you get there.” And that’s why the town was called Zoar, which means “Smalltown.”
[EN17 None] § Total Destruction
[EN18 23] The sun was already high in the sky when Lot finally got to Zoar.
[EN19 24-25] Then God made it rain fire and brimstone on Sodom and Gomorrah. It was like a river of lava pouring down from the sky! He destroyed the cities, the whole plain, every single person living there, and everything that grew in the ground.
[EN20 26] But Lot’s wife looked back, and she instantly turned into a pillar of salt.
[EN21 27-28] Abraham got up early the next morning and went to the spot where he had stood with God. He looked out over Sodom and Gomorrah and the whole plain, and all he saw was a massive cloud of smoke rising from the earth, like smoke from a giant furnace.
[EN22 29] So that’s the story. When God destroyed the cities on the plain, he remembered his boy Abraham and made sure to get Lot out of there before he completely obliterated those cities.
[EN23 None] § A Desperate Plan
[EN24 30] Lot was too scared to stay in Zoar, so he left and went to live in the mountains with his two daughters. They ended up living in a cave.
[EN25 31-32] One day, the older daughter said to the younger one, “Dad’s getting old, and there are literally no men left in this whole area for us to have kids with. Let’s get Dad drunk and then sleep with him. It’s the only way we can have kids and keep our family line going.”
[EN26 33-35] So that night, they got their father drunk. The older daughter went in and slept with him, and he was so out of it he had no idea what happened. The next morning, the older sister told the younger one, “Okay, I slept with Dad last night. Tonight, it’s your turn. We’ll get him drunk again, and you go sleep with him. That way, we’ll both have a kid through our dad and our family won’t die out.”
[EN27 35-36] So they got their father drunk again that night, and the younger daughter went in and slept with him. And again, he was totally oblivious and didn’t know a thing.
[EN28 37-38] Both of Lot’s daughters ended up getting pregnant by their own father. The older daughter had a son and named him Moab, and he’s the ancestor of the Moabites today. The younger daughter also had a son and named him Ben-Ammi, and he’s the ancestor of the Ammonites.
