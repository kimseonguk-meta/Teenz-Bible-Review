===== CHAPTER 42: Joseph's Brothers Face a Surprise Test (27 EN, 27 KO, 23 MSG) =====
[EN0 1-2] When Jacob found out there was food in Egypt, he was like, 'Guys, why are you just sitting around staring at each other? I heard there's food in Egypt. Get down there and buy some so we don't starve.'
[EN1 3-5] So, ten of Joseph's brothers went to Egypt to get food. But Jacob didn't let Joseph's younger brother, Benjamin, go with them. He was scared something bad might happen to him. So, the sons of Israel went with everyone else to Egypt to buy food because the famine was hitting Canaan hard, too.
[EN2 None] § Joseph's Surprise Reunion
[EN3 6-7] Joseph was basically running the show in Egypt, handing out food to everyone. When his brothers showed up, they bowed down to him, showing major respect. Joseph knew who they were right away, but he acted like they were total strangers and was harsh with them.
[EN4 7] He asked, 'Where are you from?'
[EN5 7] 'From Canaan,' they replied. 'We're here to buy food.'
[EN6 8-9] Joseph knew it was them, but they had no clue who he was. He remembered the dreams he had about them and said, 'You're spies! You're here to find our weak spots.'
[EN7 10-11] 'No way, sir,' they said. 'We're just here to buy food. We're all brothers from the same family. We're honest guys, we'd never spy on you.'
[EN8 12] 'Nah, you're spies,' he insisted. 'You're here to check out our weaknesses.'
[EN9 13] They explained, 'There were twelve of us brothers, all sons of the same dad in Canaan. The youngest is back home with our father, and one of us is... gone.'
[EN10 14-16] But Joseph wasn't buying it. 'It's just like I said, you're spies. And here's how I'm gonna prove it. I swear by the Pharaoh, you're not leaving this place until your younger brother gets here. One of you has to go get him while the rest of you stay in jail. We'll see if you're telling the truth. I swear by the Pharaoh, you're spies.'
[EN11 17] And with that, he threw them in jail for three days.
[EN12 None] § The Test
[EN13 18-20] On the third day, Joseph told them, 'Do this and you'll live. I'm a man who fears God. If you're as honest as you claim, one of you will stay here in jail while the rest of you take food back to your starving families. But you have to bring your youngest brother back to me. That'll prove you're telling the truth, and you won't die.' They agreed.
[EN14 21] Then they started talking to each other, saying, 'This is payback for what we did to our brother. We saw how scared he was when he begged for mercy, but we didn't listen. Now we're the ones in deep trouble.'
[EN15 22] Reuben jumped in, 'Didn't I tell you not to hurt the kid? But you wouldn't listen. And now we're paying for his murder.'
[EN16 23-24] Joseph had been using an interpreter, so they didn't know he understood every word. He had to turn away from them and cry. When he got himself together, he took Simeon and had him tied up right in front of them.
[EN17 25] Then Joseph ordered his servants to fill their sacks with grain, put their money back in each sack, and give them food for their journey. It was all done for them.
[EN18 26] They loaded up their donkeys with the food and left.
[EN19 None] § The Money's Back?
[EN20 27-28] When they stopped for the night, one of them opened his sack to feed his donkey and found his money right at the top of the bag. He yelled to his brothers, 'My money's back! It's right here in my bag!' They were all confused and scared. 'What is God doing to us?' they wondered.
[EN21 29-32] When they got back to their father, Jacob, in Canaan, they told him everything that had happened. 'The man in charge of the country was really rough with us and accused us of being spies. We told him, 'We're honest guys, not spies. There were twelve of us brothers, sons of one father. One is gone, and the youngest is with our dad in Canaan.'
[EN22 33-34] 'But the man in charge said, 'Leave one of your brothers with me, take food for your starving families, and go. Bring your youngest brother back to me to prove you're honest and not spies. Then I'll give your brother back to you, and you can come and go as you please in this country.''
[EN23 35] As they were emptying their sacks, each man found his pouch of money. When they and their father saw the money, they were all freaked out.
[EN24 36] Their father, Jacob, said to them, 'You're taking everything from me! Joseph is gone, Simeon is gone, and now you want to take Benjamin. You're going to leave me with nothing!'
[EN25 37] Reuben spoke up, 'I'll give you my two sons as hostages. If I don't bring Benjamin back, you can kill them. Trust me with him; I'll bring him back.'
[EN26 38] But Jacob refused. 'My son is not going with you. His brother is dead, and he's all I have left. If something bad happens to him on the way, you'll be the death of me.'
