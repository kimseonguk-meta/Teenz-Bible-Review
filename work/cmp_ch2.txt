This is the story of how it all began, how the heavens and the earth were created.
[EN2 badge=None] § Adam and Eve
[EN3 badge=5-7] Back when God made the earth and the heavens, there were no plants or anything growing yet. That’s because God hadn't sent any rain, and there was no one to even farm the land. The whole planet was watered by these underground springs. Then, God took some dirt from the ground, shaped it into a man, and breathed life into him. And just like that, the man became a living person!
[EN4 badge=8-9] Then God planted this awesome garden in a place called Eden, out in the east. He put the man He had just made in it. God made all sorts of cool trees grow there—trees that were nice to look at and had delicious fruit. In the middle of the garden was the Tree of Life, and also the Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil.

[EN6 badge=15] God put the man in the Garden of Eden to take care of it and keep it in good shape.
[EN7 badge=16-17] God gave the man a command, “You can eat from any tree in the garden, for real. But, you can’t eat from the Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil. Don’t do it. The second you eat from that tree, you’re dead. Seriously.”
[EN8 badge=18-21] God said, “It’s not good for the man to be all by himself. I’ll make him a helper, a real partner.” So God made all the animals and birds out of the ground. He brought them to the man to see what he would name them. Whatever the man called each creature, that was its name. The man gave names to all the cattle, the birds, and the wild animals, but he still couldn’t find a good partner for himself.
[EN9 badge=22-23] So God made the man fall into a super deep sleep. While he was sleeping, God took out one of his ribs and patched him up. Then God used that rib to make a woman and brought her to the man.
[EN10 badge=24-25] The man was like, “Whoa, finally! She’s like me, bone of my bone, flesh of my flesh! I’ll call her Woman, because she was made from Man.” That’s why a man leaves his parents and sticks with his wife, and they become one. The two of them, the man and his wife, were both naked, but they weren’t embarrassed or anything.
