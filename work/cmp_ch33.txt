===== CHAPTER 33: Jacob and Esau: The Ultimate Reunion (18 EN, 18 KO, 12 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § The Reunion
[EN1 1-3] Jacob looked up and boom, there was Esau, rolling up with 400 guys. Jacob quickly split up his kids with Leah, Rachel, and the two maids. He put the maids and their kids in the front, Leah and her kids in the middle, and Rachel and Joseph at the back.
[EN2 3-4] Jacob went ahead of everyone. As he got closer to his brother, he bowed down seven times to show respect. But Esau just ran to him, threw his arms around him, and kissed him. They both started crying, for real.
[EN3 None] § Family Introductions
[EN4 5] Then Esau looked around at the women and kids and was like, "Yo, who's all this with you?"
Jacob said, "These are the kids God was awesome enough to give me."
[EN5 6-7] So the maids and their kids came up and bowed. Then Leah and her kids did the same. Finally, Joseph and Rachel came up and bowed to Esau.
[EN6 None] § What's With All the Gifts?
[EN7 8] Esau then asked, "So what was up with all those herds of animals I saw on the way?"
Jacob was like, "I was kinda hoping they'd make you happy to see me."
[EN8 9] But Esau said, "Bro, for real, I have more than enough. Keep your stuff."
[EN9 10-11] Jacob said, "No, please, for real. If you're actually cool with me, take these gifts. Seeing your face is like seeing God's face smiling at me. Seriously, take them. God's been so good to me, I have everything I need." Jacob kept insisting, so Esau finally took the gifts.
[EN10 None] § Going Their Separate Ways
[EN11 12] Then Esau said, "Alright, let's get going. I'll lead the way."
[EN12 13-14] But Jacob said, "My dude, you can see the kids are tired. Plus, the animals are nursing, so we gotta go slow. If I push them too hard for even one day, they'll all die. So, how about you go on ahead, and I'll just take it easy at my own pace with the flocks and the kids. I'll meet you in Seir."
[EN13 15] Esau was like, "Okay, but at least let me leave some of my guys with you."
"Nah, it's all good," Jacob said. "You being cool with me is all I need."
[EN14 16] So Esau headed back to Seir that day.
[EN15 None] § Jacob's New Crib
[EN16 17] And Jacob left for a place called Succoth. He built a house for himself and some sheds for his animals. That's why they called the place Succoth, which means "Sheds."
[EN17 18-20] After all that, Jacob finally made it to Shechem in Canaan, all the way from Paddan Aram, totally safe. He set up his camp near the city. He bought the piece of land where he pitched his tent from the sons of Hamor, who was Shechem's dad. It cost him a hundred silver coins. Then he built an altar there and called it El-Elohe-Israel, which means "The God of Israel is freaking awesome."
