===== CHAPTER 32: Jacob Wrestles God and Gets a New Name (24 EN, 24 KO, 17 MSG) =====
[EN0 1-2] So Jacob continued his journey, and suddenly, a bunch of God's angels showed up. When Jacob saw them, he was like, “Whoa, God’s squad is here!” So he named the place Mahanaim, which means “Two Camps.”
[EN1 None] § Jacob's Big "Uh Oh" Moment
[EN2 3-5] Jacob sent some messengers ahead to his brother Esau, who was in the Seir region of Edom. He told them, “Give my master Esau this message from your servant Jacob: ‘I’ve been hanging with Laban this whole time and just couldn't get away. I’ve got a ton of cattle, donkeys, sheep, and servants now. I’m just letting you know, hoping you’ll be cool with me.’”
[EN3 6] The messengers came back to Jacob and said, “We talked to your brother Esau, and he’s on his way to meet you. But, uh... he’s got 400 men with him.”
[EN4 7-8] Jacob was totally freaking out. Panicked, he split all his people, sheep, cattle, and camels into two groups. His thinking was, “If Esau attacks the first group, at least the other one has a shot at getting away.”
[EN5 None] § The Ultimate Prayer
[EN6 9-12] Then Jacob just prayed his heart out, “God of my grandpa Abraham, God of my dad Isaac, the same God who told me, ‘Go back home, and I’ll take care of you.’ I seriously don’t deserve all the awesome love and faithfulness you’ve shown me. When I left here, I had nothing but the clothes on my back, and now look at me—I’ve got two whole camps! Please, save me from my brother, from my angry brother Esau! I’m terrified he’s going to come and wipe us all out, me, the moms, and the kids. You promised me
[EN7 None] § Operation: Appease Esau
[EN8 13-15] He slept there that night and then got a massive gift ready for his brother Esau from everything he owned: 200 female goats, 20 male goats, 200 ewes, 20 rams, 30 nursing camels with their babies, 40 cows, 10 bulls, 20 female donkeys, and 10 male donkeys. He put a servant in charge of each herd and told them, “Go on ahead of me, but leave some space between each herd.”
[EN9 16-18] He told the first servant, “When my brother Esau meets you and asks, ‘Who’s your boss? Where are you going? Who owns all this?’ you tell him, ‘This all belongs to your servant Jacob. It’s a gift for his master, Esau. And Jacob himself is right behind us.’”
[EN10 19-21] He gave the same instructions to the second and third servants, and to all the others with the herds. He thought, “I’ll chill him out with all these gifts coming one after another. Then, when I see him face-to-face, maybe he’ll actually be happy to see me.” So, the gifts went on ahead, and he stayed in the camp for the night.
[EN11 None] § The Mysterious Wrestling Match
[EN12 22-23] But in the middle of the night, he got up, took his two wives, his two maids, and his eleven kids, and crossed the Jabbok River. He made sure they all got across the river safely with all his stuff.
[EN13 24-25] But Jacob stayed behind by himself. And then some guy started wrestling with him, and they went at it until sunrise. When the guy saw he couldn’t win against Jacob, he touched Jacob’s hip and popped it out of its socket.
[EN14 26] The man said, “Let me go, the sun’s coming up.”
[EN15 26] But Jacob shot back, “I’m not letting you go until you bless me.”
[EN16 27] The man asked, “What’s your name?”
[EN17 27] He said, “Jacob.”
[EN18 28] Then the man said, “Not anymore. Your name isn’t Jacob anymore. From now on, you’re Israel, which means ‘God-Wrestler,’ because you’ve wrestled with God and with humans, and you’ve won.”
[EN19 29] Jacob then asked, “Okay, so what’s your name?”
[EN20 29] The man replied, “Why do you even need to know my name?” And right then and there, he blessed him.
[EN21 None] § A New Name and a New Limp
[EN22 30-31] Jacob named that place Peniel, meaning “God’s Face,” because he said, “I saw God face-to-face, and I’m still alive to talk about it! How crazy is that?”
[EN23 31-32] The sun rose just as he was leaving Peniel, and he was limping because of his hip. That’s why, to this day, the people of Israel don’t eat the muscle on the hip socket, because that’s where Jacob was injured.
