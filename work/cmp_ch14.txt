===== CHAPTER 14: Abram's Epic Rescue Mission (18 EN, 18 KO, 9 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § The Great King War
[EN1 1-2] So check it, four kings—Amraphel from Shinar, Arioch from Ellasar, Kedorlaomer from Elam, and Tidal from Goiim—decided to roll out and start a war. Their targets? Five other kings: Bera from Sodom, Birsha from Gomorrah, Shinab from Admah, Shemeber from Zeboiim, and the king of Bela (also known as Zoar).
[EN2 3-4] These five kings had been totally owned by Kedorlaomer for twelve years. But in the thirteenth year, they were like, "Nah, we're done," and they rebelled. All these guys met up to fight in the Siddim Valley, which is now the Salt Sea.
[EN3 None] § Kedorlaomer Strikes Back
[EN4 5-7] A year later, Kedorlaomer and his crew came back with a vengeance. They went on a total rampage, crushing the Rephaim, the Zuzim, the Emim, and the Horites. They pushed all the way to the edge of the desert. On their way back, they wrecked the whole area of the Amalekites and the Amorites.
[EN5 8-10] That's when the five-king squad marched out to face them. It was a 4-on-5 battle in the Siddim Valley. But the valley was a mess, full of sticky tar pits. When the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah tried to escape, they fell right into the pits. The rest of their guys scattered and ran for the mountains.
[EN6 11-12] The four kings took everything from Sodom and Gomorrah—all their stuff, all their food, everything. And to make it worse, they also kidnapped Lot, Abram's nephew who was living in Sodom, and took all his possessions too.
[EN7 None] § Abram's Rescue Mission
[EN8 13] Someone who escaped the battle ran and told Abram what happened. Abram was chilling by the oaks of Mamre the Amorite. Mamre and his brothers, Eshcol and Aner, were Abram's allies.
[EN9 14-15] When Abram heard his nephew was captured, he didn't hesitate. He gathered his 318 trained guys, all born in his household, and went after the enemy, chasing them all the way to Dan. That night, Abram and his squad split up, attacked from different sides, and totally surprised them. They chased the enemy all the way to Hobah, north of Damascus.
[EN10 16] They got everything back—all the stolen stuff, Abram's nephew Lot, his belongings, and all the captured people, including the women.
[EN11 None] § A Royal Blessing
[EN12 17-18] After Abram came back from this epic win, the king of Sodom came out to greet him. Then this other king, Melchizedek of Salem, who was a priest of the Most High God, showed up with bread and wine. He blessed Abram, saying:
[EN13 19-20] "May Abram be blessed by the Most High God, the Creator of heaven and earth. And praise to the Most High God, who gave you this victory over your enemies."
[EN14 20] It was a huge moment. Abram was so grateful that he gave Melchizedek a tenth of everything he had recovered.
[EN15 None] § Abram Keeps It Real
[EN16 21] The king of Sodom then told Abram, "Hey, just give me my people back. You can keep all the loot for yourself."
[EN17 22-24] But Abram was like, "Nah, I'm good. I made a promise to the Most High God, the Creator of heaven and earth, that I wouldn't take a single thing from you, not even a thread or a shoelace. I don't want you ever saying, 'I'm the one who made Abram rich.' My guys already ate, so that's fine. And my allies, Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre, they should get their fair share. But for me? I'm not taking anything."
