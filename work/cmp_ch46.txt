===== CHAPTER 46: The Epic Family Road Trip to Egypt (29 EN, 29 KO, 24 MSG) =====
[EN0 1] So, Israel packed up everything he owned and started his journey. He got to Beersheba and took a moment to worship, offering sacrifices to the God of his father, Isaac.
[EN1 2] That night, God spoke to Israel in a vision, calling out, “Jacob! Jacob!”
[EN2 2] “Yeah?” he answered. “I’m listening.”
[EN3 3-4] God said, “I am the God of your father. Don’t be scared about going down to Egypt. I’m going to turn you into a huge nation there. I’ll go down to Egypt with you, and I’ll also bring you back. And when you die, Joseph will be right there with you; he’ll be the one to close your eyes.”
[EN4 None] § The Big Move
[EN5 5-7] So Jacob left Beersheba. Israel’s sons loaded up their father, their little kids, and their wives onto the wagons that Pharaoh had sent for him. They rolled into Egypt with all their livestock and the stuff they had gathered in Canaan. Jacob brought his entire family with him—sons and grandsons, daughters and granddaughters. Literally everyone.
[EN6 None] § The Whole Crew
[EN7 8] Here’s the list of the Israelites, Jacob and his descendants, who went to Egypt:
[EN8 9] Reuben, Jacob’s firstborn. Reuben’s sons were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.
[EN9 10] Simeon’s sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jakin, Zohar, and Shaul, whose mom was a Canaanite woman.
[EN10 11] Levi’s sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.
[EN11 12] Judah’s sons were Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah (though Er and Onan had already died back in Canaan). Perez’s sons were Hezron and Hamul.
[EN12 13] Issachar’s sons were Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron.
[EN13 14] Zebulun’s sons were Sered, Elon, and Jahleel.
[EN14 15] These were the sons Leah had with Jacob in Paddan Aram, plus his daughter Dinah. In total, that’s thirty-three sons and daughters.
[EN15 16] Gad’s sons were Zephon, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli.
[EN16 17] Asher’s sons were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah. They also had a sister, Serah. Beriah’s sons were Heber and Malkiel.
[EN17 18] These sixteen were the children Jacob had with Zilpah, the servant Laban had given to his daughter Leah.
[EN18 19-21] The sons of Jacob’s wife Rachel were Joseph and Benjamin. Joseph had two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, with his wife Asenath, who was the daughter of Potiphera, a priest of On. They were born in Egypt. Benjamin’s sons were Bela, Beker, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard.
[EN19 22] These fourteen were the children Jacob had through Rachel.
[EN20 23] Dan’s son was Hushim.
[EN21 24] Naphtali’s sons were Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem.
[EN22 25] These seven were the children Jacob had through Bilhah, the servant Laban had given to his daughter Rachel.
[EN23 26-27] All in all, the number of people who went to Egypt with Jacob—his direct descendants, not counting his sons’ wives—was sixty-six. If you add the two sons born to Joseph in Egypt, Jacob’s family in Egypt totaled seventy people.
[EN24 None] § The Reunion
[EN25 28-29] Jacob sent Judah ahead to get directions to Goshen from Joseph. When they arrived in the Goshen area, Joseph got his chariot ready and went to meet his father, Israel. The moment Joseph saw him, he threw his arms around his neck and just wept for a long time.
[EN26 30] Israel said to Joseph, “I’m ready to die now. I’ve seen your face—you’re actually alive.”
