===== CHAPTER 30: Babies, Mandrakes, and a Flock of Riches (38 EN, 38 KO, 17 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § The Baby War Begins
[EN1 1] When Rachel realized she wasn't having any kids for Jacob, she got crazy jealous of her sister, Leah. She told Jacob, "You have to give me sons, or I'll literally die!"
[EN2 2] Jacob got pretty mad and shot back, "Hey, am I God? I'm not the one stopping you from having babies!"
[EN3 3-4] So Rachel came up with a plan. "Okay, look, here's my servant, Bilhah. Sleep with her. She can be a surrogate for me, and I can start a family through her." So she gave Bilhah to Jacob, and he slept with her. Bilhah got pregnant and had a son for Jacob.
[EN4 5-6] Rachel said, "God totally had my back and proved I was right. He heard me and gave me a son!" So she named him Dan, which means 'Vindicated.'
[EN5 7-8] Then, Rachel's servant Bilhah got pregnant again and gave Jacob a second son. Rachel was like, "I've been in a legit wrestling match with my sister, and I WON." So she named him Naphtali, which means 'My Struggle.'
[EN6 None] § Leah Fights Back
[EN7 9] When Leah saw she wasn't having any more kids, she pulled the same move. She gave her servant Zilpah to Jacob as a wife. Zilpah had a son for Jacob.
[EN8 10-11] Leah exclaimed, "Wow, so lucky!" and she named him Gad, which means 'Good Fortune.'
[EN9 12-13] When Leah's servant Zilpah had a second son, Leah said, "This is such a happy day! All the women are gonna congratulate me on how happy I am." So she named him Asher, which means 'Happy.'
[EN10 None] § The Mandrake Deal
[EN11 14] One day, during the wheat harvest, Leah's son Reuben found some mandrakes in a field and brought them home to his mom. Rachel saw them and asked Leah, "Hey, can I please have some of those mandrakes your son found?"
[EN12 15] Leah was not having it. "Wasn't it bad enough that you stole my husband from me? And now you want my son's mandrakes too?"
[EN13 15-16] Rachel shot back, "Fine. Here's the deal: I'll let Jacob sleep with you tonight if you give me your son's mandrakes."
[EN14 16] So when Jacob came home from the fields that evening, Leah was waiting for him. "You're sleeping with me tonight. I traded my son's mandrakes for a night with you." So he slept with her that night.
[EN15 17-18] And God listened to Leah. She got pregnant and gave Jacob a fifth son. She said, "God rewarded me for giving my servant to my husband." She named him Issachar, which means 'Reward.'
[EN16 19-20] Leah got pregnant again and had a sixth son for Jacob. She said, "God has given me an awesome gift. This time my husband will finally respect me—I've given him six sons!" So she named him Zebulun, which means 'Honor.'
[EN17 21] Finally, she had a daughter and named her Dinah.
[EN18 None] § God Remembers Rachel
[EN19 22] Then, God finally remembered Rachel. He listened to her prayers and made it possible for her to have a baby. She got pregnant and had a son!
[EN20 23-24] She said, "God has taken away my shame." She named him Joseph, which means 'Add,' and prayed, "I hope God adds another son to me!"
[EN21 None] § Jacob Wants to Go Home
[EN22 25-26] After Rachel had Joseph, Jacob went to Laban and said, "It's time. Let me go back to my own home. Give me my wives and my kids that I worked for. You know exactly how hard I've worked for you."
[EN23 27-28] Laban replied, "Please, don't go. I had it confirmed by a divine source that God has blessed me like crazy because of you." He added, "So just name your price. Whatever you want, I'll pay you."
[EN24 29-30] Jacob said, "You know firsthand what my work has meant to you and how your livestock have thrived under my care. You had almost nothing when I got here, and now you're loaded. Everything I touched turned into a blessing for you. Isn't it about time I start providing for my own family?"
[EN25 None] § The Deal for the Flock
[EN26 31] "Okay, so what should I pay you?" Laban asked.
[EN27 32] Jacob answered, "You don't have to pay me anything. But how about this deal? I'll go back to taking care of your flocks. Today, go through all your flocks and pull out every sheep that's speckled or spotted, every dark-colored lamb, and every goat that's spotted or speckled. That will be my payment.
[EN28 33] "This way, you can easily check if I'm being honest. Later, when you check on my wages, if you find any goat that isn't speckled or spotted, or any sheep that isn't black, you'll know I stole it."
[EN29 34] "Fair enough," said Laban. "It's a deal."
[EN30 None] § Laban's Scheme vs. Jacob's Genius
[EN31 35] But that same day, Laban went and removed all the male goats that were mottled and spotted, and all the female goats that were speckled and spotted—basically any animal with a speck of white on it—plus all the black sheep. He put them in his sons' care.
[EN32 36] Then he made sure there was a three-day journey separating him from Jacob. Meanwhile, Jacob was left taking care of the rest of Laban's flock.
[EN33 37] But Jacob was a genius. He got some fresh branches from poplar, almond, and plane trees and peeled strips of bark off them, creating white stripes. He then stuck these peeled branches in the watering troughs right where the flocks came to drink.
[EN34 38-39] When the animals were in heat, they'd come to drink and end up mating right in front of the striped branches. And guess what? They gave birth to babies that were streaked, spotted, or speckled!
[EN35 40] Jacob also separated the ewes and made them face the dark-colored animals from Laban's original flock. By doing this, he created his own distinct flocks that he didn't mix with Laban's.
[EN36 41-42] Whenever the stronger, healthier animals were mating, Jacob would put the branches in the troughs so they'd mate in front of them. But he didn't put the branches out for the weaker animals. This way, the weak animals went to Laban, and all the strong ones went to Jacob.
[EN37 43] The guy got richer and richer. He ended up with huge flocks, tons of servants, and a whole lot of camels and donkeys.
