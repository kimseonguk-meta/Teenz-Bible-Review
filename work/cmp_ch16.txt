===== CHAPTER 16: The OG Surrogate Mom Drama (10 EN, 9 KO, 9 MSG) =====
[EN0 1-3] So, Abram's wife, Sarai, still hadn't had any kids. She had this Egyptian servant girl named Hagar. One day, Sarai tells Abram, 'Look, God's obviously not letting me have a baby. So, go sleep with my servant. Maybe I can start a family through her.' And Abram was like, 'Alright, bet.'
[EN1 3-4] So Sarai, Abram's wife, literally gave her Egyptian servant Hagar to her husband Abram to be like, a second wife. This all went down after Abram had been living in Canaan for ten years. He slept with Hagar, and boom, she got pregnant. The second Hagar found out she was pregnant, she started acting all high and mighty, totally dissing Sarai.
[EN2 5] Sarai went straight to Abram and was like, 'This is all your fault! I'm getting treated like dirt here. I give you my servant, and the moment she gets pregnant, she acts like I'm nothing. Let God figure out who's in the right.'
[EN3 6] 'She's your servant, you handle it,' Abram said. So Sarai started being really harsh to Hagar, and Hagar just ran away.
[EN4 None] §Hagar on the Run
[EN5 7-8] An angel of God found her by a spring out in the desert on the road to Shur. The angel asked, 'Hagar, Sarai's servant, what are you doing out here?' She answered, 'I'm running away from my boss, Sarai.'
[EN6 9-12] The angel of God told her, 'Go back to your boss and just deal with her. And listen, I'm going to give you so many descendants, you won't even be able to count them all. You're going to have a son, and you need to name him Ishmael, because God heard you when you were in trouble. He's gonna be a total wild card, a real fighter, always getting into it with people. He'll be stirring up drama with his own family all the time.'
[EN7 13] She gave a name to the God who spoke to her: 'You are the God who sees me.' She was like, 'OMG, for real? I just saw the one who sees me!'
[EN8 14] That's legit how the spring got its name: Beer-lahai-roi, which means 'The Well of the Living One Who Sees Me.' It's still there, between Kadesh and Bered.
[EN9 15-16] So, Hagar had Abram's son, and Abram named him Ishmael. Abram was 86 years old when Hagar gave birth to Ishmael.

[MSG0 16] 1-2 Sarai, Abram’s wife, hadn’t yet produced a child. She had an Egyptian maid named Hagar. Sarai said to Abram, “God has not seen fit to let me have a child. Sleep with my maid. Maybe I can get a family from her.” Abram agreed to do what Sarai said.
[MSG1 3-4] So Sarai, Abram’s wife, took her Egyptian maid Hagar and gave her to her husband Abram as a wife. Abram had been living ten years in Canaan when this took place. He slept with Hagar and she got pregnant. When Hagar learned she was pregnant, she looked down on her mistress.
[MSG2 5] Sarai told Abram, “It’s all your fault that I’m suffering this abuse. I put my maid in bed with you and the minute she knows she’s pregnant, she treats me like I’m nothing. May God decide which of us is right.”
[MSG3 6] “You decide,” said Abram. “Your maid is your business.” Sarai was abusive to Hagar and Hagar ran away.
[MSG4 7-8] An angel of God found her beside a spring in the desert; it was the spring on the road to Shur. He said, “Hagar, maid of Sarai, what are you doing here?” She said, “I’m running away from Sarai my mistress.”
[MSG5 9-12] The angel of God said, “Go back to your mistress. Put up with her abuse.” He continued, “I’m going to give you a big family, children past counting. From this pregnancy, you’ll get a son: Name him Ishmael;     for God heard you, God answered you.  He’ll be a bucking bronco of a man,     a real fighter, fighting and being fought,  Always stirring up trouble,     always at odds with his family.”
[MSG6 13] She answered God by name, praying to the God who spoke to her, “You’re the God who sees me! “Yes! He saw me; and then I saw him!”
[MSG7 14] That’s how that desert spring got named “God-Alive-Sees-Me Spring.” That spring is still there, between Kadesh and Bered.
[MSG8 15-16] Hagar gave Abram a son. Abram named him Ishmael. Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar gave him his son, Ishmael.
