===== CHAPTER 25: A Birthright Traded for a Bowl of Stew (17 EN, 17 KO, 16 MSG) =====
[EN0 1-4] So, Abraham got married again to a woman named Keturah. They had a bunch of sons: Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. Jokshan's kids were Sheba and Dedan, and Dedan's descendants were the Asshurim, Letushim, and Leummim. Midian's sons were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah—all from Keturah's side of the family.
[EN1 5-6] But here's the deal: Abraham gave literally everything he owned to his son, Isaac. As for the sons he had with his other wives, he gave them some nice gifts while he was still alive, but then sent them packing to the east. He wanted to make sure there was plenty of space between them and his main heir, Isaac.
[EN2 7-11] Abraham lived a super long life, 175 years. Then, he took his last breath and died peacefully. He was really old but had lived a full, happy life. His sons, Isaac and Ishmael, buried him in the cave of Machpelah, in the field Abraham had bought from the Hittites. He was buried right next to his wife, Sarah. After Abraham passed away, God totally blessed his son Isaac, who was living at a place called Beer Lahai Roi.
[EN3 None] § Ishmael's Crew
[EN4 12-16] Now for Ishmael's family tree. Remember him? He was Abraham's son with Hagar, Sarah's Egyptian maid. Here are Ishmael's sons, in the order they were born: Nebaioth (the oldest), Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. Their towns and camps were all named after them, making twelve princes for twelve tribes.
[EN5 17-18] Ishmael lived to be 137. When he died, he was buried with his family. His descendants settled in the area from Havilah all the way to Shur, which is east of Egypt toward Assyria. The thing is, the Ishmaelites pretty much didn't get along with any of their relatives.
[EN6 None] § The Twin Rivalry Begins
[EN7 19-20] Okay, now for Isaac's family story. Abraham had Isaac, and when Isaac was 40, he married Rebekah. She was the daughter of Bethuel the Aramean and the sister of Laban the Aramean.
[EN8 21-22] Rebekah couldn't have kids, so Isaac prayed super hard to God for her. God answered his prayer, and Rebekah got pregnant. But the babies inside her were fighting and kicking like crazy. She was like, "If this is what pregnancy is like, why am I even alive?" So she went to ask God what was going on.
[EN9 23] God told her, "You've got two nations inside you, two peoples who are already rivals in your womb. One will be stronger than the other, and the older son will end up serving the younger one."
[EN10 24-26] When it was time to give birth, it turned out she was having twins, for real. The first one came out all red and covered in hair, like he was wearing a fuzzy blanket. They named him Esau, which means "Hairy." His brother came out right after, grabbing onto Esau's heel, so they named him Jacob, which means "Heel-grabber." Isaac was 60 years old when they were born.
[EN11 None] § A Birthright for a Bowl of Stew
[EN12 27-28] The boys grew up. Esau became a skilled hunter, a total outdoors guy. Jacob was more of a quiet dude who preferred to stay inside. Isaac's favorite was Esau because he loved the wild game Esau brought home, but Rebekah's favorite was Jacob.
[EN13 29-30] One day, Jacob was cooking up some stew when Esau came in from the fields, totally starving. Esau said to Jacob, "Quick, give me some of that red stew! I'm dying of hunger!" (That's actually how he got the nickname Edom, which means "Red.")
[EN14 31] But Jacob said, "Okay, but you have to trade me. Give me your rights as the firstborn son."
[EN15 32] Esau was like, "Dude, I'm about to starve to death! What good are birthright privileges if I'm dead?"
[EN16 33-34] Jacob insisted, "Swear to me first." So Esau did. He swore an oath and traded away his birthright. Then Jacob gave him some bread and the lentil stew. Esau ate, drank, got up, and left. That's how little he cared about his special rights as the firstborn. It was a total shrug-off.
