===== CHAPTER 23: Abraham Buys a Forever Home for Sarah (11 EN, 11 KO, 9 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § A Place to Bury Sarah
[EN1 1-2] Sarah lived to be 127 years old. She died in a place called Kiriath Arba (which is now Hebron) in the land of Canaan. Abraham was obviously heartbroken and cried a lot.
[EN2 3-4] After a while, Abraham got up and went to talk to the local Hittites. He was like, "Yo, I know I'm just a foreigner here, but could you sell me a piece of land for a burial plot? I need to give my wife a proper burial."
[EN3 5-6] The Hittites were super cool about it and said, "Dude, you're not just some random outsider. You're like a prince of God to us! You can bury your wife in the best burial spot we have. Seriously, nobody here would say no to you."
[EN4 None] § Buying the Field
[EN5 7-9] Abraham was really grateful and bowed to them. He said, "If you guys are for real about helping me, could you do me a solid and talk to Ephron, Zohar's son? Ask him to sell me the cave of Machpelah at the end of his field. I'll pay full price for it, right here in front of you all as witnesses."
[EN6 10-11] Ephron, who was one of the Hittite leaders, spoke up in front of everyone, "No way, my dude! I can't do that. But look, the field is yours. It's a gift. I'm giving you the field and the cave. Everyone here is a witness. Go ahead and bury your wife."
[EN7 12-13] Abraham bowed again to everyone and said to Ephron, "Please, let me pay for it. Just take the money so I can bury my wife."
[EN8 14-15] Ephron replied, "Look, if you're gonna insist... what's 400 silver coins between friends? Just go and bury your wife."
[EN9 16] So, Abraham agreed and paid Ephron the 400 silver shekels, using the standard rate everyone agreed on.
[EN10 17-20] And that's the story of how Ephron's field near Mamre—the field, the cave, and all the trees—officially became Abraham's property. The Hittite council witnessed the whole deal. Then Abraham buried his wife Sarah in the cave on that field. The land and the cave were officially transferred from the Hittites to Abraham as his own burial plot.
----- MSG -----
[MSG0 23] 1-2 Sarah lived 127 years. Sarah died in Kiriath Arba, present-day Hebron, in the land of Canaan. Abraham mourned for Sarah and wept.
[MSG1 3-4] Then Abraham got up from mourning his dead wife and spoke to the Hittites: “I know I’m only an outsider here among you, but sell me a burial plot so that I can bury my dead decently.”
[MSG2 5-6] The Hittites responded, “Why, you’re no mere outsider here with us, you’re a prince of God! Bury your dead wife in the best of our burial sites. None of us will refuse you a place for burial.”
[MSG3 7-9] Then Abraham got up, bowed respectfully to the people of the land, the Hittites, and said, “If you’re serious about helping me give my wife a proper burial, intercede for me with Ephron son of Zohar. Ask him to sell me the cave of Machpelah that he owns, the one at the end of his land. Ask him to sell it to me at its full price for a burial plot, with you as witnesses.”
[MSG4 10-11] Ephron was part of the local Hittite community. Then Ephron the Hittite spoke up, answering Abraham with all the Hittites who were part of the town council listening: “Oh no, my master! I couldn’t do that. The field is yours—a gift. I’ll give it and the cave to you. With my people as witnesses, I give it to you. Bury your deceased wife.”
[MSG5 12-13] Abraham bowed respectfully before the assembled council and answered Ephron: “Please allow me—I want to pay the price of the land; take my money so that I can go ahead and bury my wife.”
[MSG6 14-15] Then Ephron answered Abraham, “If you insist, master. What’s four hundred silver shekels between us? Now go ahead and bury your wife.”
[MSG7 16] Abraham accepted Ephron’s offer and paid out the sum that Ephron had named before the town council of Hittites—four hundred silver shekels at the current exchange rate.
[MSG8 17-20] That’s how Ephron’s field next to Mamre—the field, its cave, and all the trees within its borders—became Abraham’s property. The town council of Hittites witnessed the transaction. Abraham then proceeded to bury his wife Sarah in the cave in the field of Machpelah that is next to Mamre, present-day Hebron, in the land of Canaan. The field and its cave went from the Hittites into Abraham’s possession as a burial plot.
