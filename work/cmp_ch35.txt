===== CHAPTER 35: Jacob's Crew, New Names, and More Drama (25 EN, 25 KO, 15 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § Back to Bethel
[EN1 1] God told Jacob, "Yo, head back to Bethel. Settle down there and build an altar for the God who showed up for you when you were running from your brother Esau."
[EN2 2-3] So Jacob told his whole crew, "Alright everyone, toss out all those weird foreign gods you've got. Take a shower, put on some fresh clothes. We're heading to Bethel. I'm building an altar there for the God who had my back when I was in major trouble and has been with me this whole time."
[EN3 4-5] They handed over all their foreign god statues and even their lucky charm earrings. Jacob buried all of it under a big oak tree in Shechem. Then they bounced. A crazy fear came over all the towns around them, so nobody even tried to chase after Jacob's sons.
[EN4 6-7] Jacob and his squad finally rolled into Luz (aka Bethel) in Canaan. He built an altar and named it El-Bethel, which means 'God of Bethel,' because that's where God first appeared to him when he was escaping his brother.
[EN5 None] § A Sad Goodbye and a New Name
[EN6 8] And that's when Rebekah's old nanny, Deborah, passed away. They buried her just outside Bethel under an oak tree, and they named it "Weeping Oak."
[EN7 9-10] Then God showed up to Jacob again, right after he got back from Paddan Aram, and gave him a blessing. "Your name is Jacob, which means 'Heel-grabber,' but not anymore. From now on, you're Israel, which means 'God-Wrestler.'"
[EN8 11-12] God went on, "I'm the Almighty God. Have a ton of kids! Grow your family! A whole nation, even a bunch of nations, will come from you. Kings will be your descendants. The land I promised to Abraham and Isaac, I'm giving it to you now, and I'll pass it on to your kids after you."
[EN9 13] Then God was gone, just like that, ascending from the spot where he'd been talking to him.
[EN10 14-15] Jacob set up a stone pillar right where God had spoken to him. He poured a drink offering on it and anointed it with oil. Jacob named the place Bethel, which means 'God's House.'
[EN11 None] § A Tragic Birth
[EN12 16-17] They left Bethel. They were still a good ways from Ephrath when Rachel went into labor, and it was a really, really tough one. When the contractions were at their absolute worst, the midwife told her, "Don't worry, you've got another son!"
[EN13 18] With her last breath, because she was dying, she named him Ben-oni, which means 'Son of My Pain.' But his dad named him Benjamin, which means 'Son of Good Fortune.'
[EN14 19-20] Rachel died and was buried on the road to Ephrath, which is now Bethlehem. Jacob put up a pillar to mark her grave. It's still there today, known as "Rachel's Grave Stone."
[EN15 None] § Family Drama and a Full House
[EN16 21-22] Israel kept moving and set up camp at Migdal Eder. While they were living in that area, Reuben went and slept with his father's concubine, Bilhah. And yeah, Israel heard all about it.
[EN17 22] Jacob had twelve sons in total.
[EN18 23] The sons from Leah were: Reuben (the firstborn), Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun.
[EN19 24] The sons from Rachel were: Joseph and Benjamin.
[EN20 25] The sons from Bilhah, Rachel's servant, were: Dan and Naphtali.
[EN21 26] The sons from Zilpah, Leah's servant, were: Gad and Asher.
[EN22 26] These were all of Jacob's sons, born to him in Paddan Aram.
[EN23 None] § The End of an Era
[EN24 27-29] Finally, Jacob made it back home to his father Isaac at Mamre in Kiriath Arba, which is now Hebron, where Abraham and Isaac had both lived. Isaac was 180 years old by then. He took his last breath and died, an old man who had lived a long life. His sons, Esau and Jacob, buried him with his family.
