===== CHAPTER 9: God's Rainbow Promise and a Family Mess-Up (13 EN paras, 10 MSG paras) =====
[EN0 None] § A Fresh Start
[EN1 1-4] God gave Noah and his sons a huge blessing. He was like, "Go out there, have a ton of kids, and fill up the whole planet! Every animal, bird, and fish is gonna be scared of you. You're in charge of them now. You can eat any living creature. Just like I gave you plants, now you can have it all. But, one rule: no eating meat that still has its lifeblood in it. That's a no-go."
[EN2 5-7] "And listen up," God said, "If anyone takes a human life, I'm gonna hold them accountable—whether it's an animal or another person. Whoever kills a person, by a person they will be killed. Why? Because I made humans in my own image, to be like me. So your job is to have lots of kids and fill the Earth with life!"
[EN3 None] § The Rainbow Promise
[EN4 8-11] Then God told Noah and his sons, "I'm making a promise, a covenant, with you, your future kids, and every living thing that was on the ark with you. Here's the deal: I will never again wipe out everything on Earth with a flood. A worldwide flood? Never happening again."
[EN5 12-17] "And to prove it," God said, "I'm putting a rainbow in the clouds. It's a sign of my promise between me and the Earth. From now on, whenever I bring clouds and a rainbow shows up, I'll remember my promise. I'll see it and remember this forever-covenant between me and every living thing on the planet." God finished by saying, "This rainbow is the official sign of the promise I've made with everything on Earth."
[EN6 None] § A Drunken Mistake
[EN7 18-19] The sons who got off the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. Ham was the father of Canaan. The whole world's population came from these three guys and their families.
[EN8 20-21] Noah, who was a farmer, planted the first-ever vineyard. One day, he drank some of the wine he made, got totally drunk, and passed out naked inside his tent.
[EN9 22-23] Ham, Canaan's dad, saw his father naked. Instead of helping, he went and told his two brothers who were outside. Shem and Japheth grabbed a blanket, walked in backward without looking, and covered their dad up. They made sure not to see him naked.
[EN10 24-27] When Noah woke up from his hangover, he found out what his youngest son, Ham, had done. He was furious and said, "Cursed be Canaan! He will be the lowest of slaves to his brothers. Blessed be God, the God of Shem, but Canaan will be his slave. May God give Japheth tons of space, and may he live with Shem. But Canaan will be their slave."
[EN11 None] § The End of an Era
[EN12 28-29] Noah lived for 350 more years after the flood. In total, he lived 950 years. And then he died.
