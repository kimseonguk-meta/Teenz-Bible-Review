===== CHAPTER 13: Abram and Lot Split: God's Epic Promise (11 EN, 11 KO, 9 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § Back in the Mix
[EN1 1-4] So Abram bounced from Egypt and headed back to the Negev. He had his wife, all his stuff, and Lot was still tagging along. By this point, Abram was seriously rich—like, loaded with cattle, silver, and gold. He kept moving from the Negev, setting up camp along the way, until he got back to Bethel. That was the spot where he first pitched his tent between Bethel and Ai and built his first altar. Abram took some time there to pray to God.
[EN2 None] § Family Drama
[EN3 5-7] Lot, who was rolling with Abram, was also super rich with his own sheep, cattle, and tents. The problem was, the land couldn't handle both of them. They had way too much stuff and there just wasn't enough space. This led to some major drama, with fights breaking out between Abram's and Lot's shepherds. On top of that, the Canaanites and Perizzites were already living there, so things were crowded.
[EN4 8-9] Abram told Lot, "Look, we can't be fighting. Our shepherds shouldn't be fighting either. We're family, man. Let's just split up. The whole land is right here. If you go left, I'll go right. If you go right, I'll go left. Simple."
[EN5 None] § The Big Split
[EN6 10-12] Lot took a good look around and saw the entire Jordan plain. It was super green and well-watered, kind of like God's own garden or even like Egypt (this was before God wrecked Sodom and Gomorrah). It stretched all the way to Zoar. So, Lot was like, "I'll take that!" and he claimed the whole Jordan plain for himself, heading east.
[EN7 12-13] And that's how the uncle and nephew went their separate ways. Abram stayed in Canaan, while Lot set up his camp in the cities of the plain, getting pretty close to Sodom. Just so you know, the people of Sodom were seriously bad news—like, openly sinning against God.
[EN8 None] § God's Epic Promise
[EN9 14-17] After Lot took off, God said to Abram, "Open your eyes and look around. North, south, east, west—everything you see, this whole land, I'm giving it to you and your kids forever. I'm going to make your family so huge, you won't even be able to count them. It'll be like trying to count all the dust on the Earth. So get up and start walking. Check out the whole country, because I'm giving it all to you."
[EN10 18] So Abram packed up his tent and moved. He went and settled by the great trees of Mamre in Hebron, and right there, he built another altar to God.
