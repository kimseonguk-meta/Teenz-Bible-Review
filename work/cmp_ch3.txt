===== CHAPTER 3 =====
[EN0 None] § The First Lie
[EN1 1] So, get this: the snake was the craftiest of all the wild animals God had made. He slithered up to the woman and was like, "Yo, did God really say you can't eat from any of the trees in the garden?"
[EN2 2-3] The woman shot back, "Nah, that's not it. We can totally eat from the trees in the garden. It's just that one tree in the middle. God was like, 'Don't eat from it, don't even touch it, or you'll die.'"
[EN3 4-5] The snake hissed, "Pfft, you're not gonna die. God's just saying that because he knows the second you eat it, your eyes will be opened. You'll be like God, knowing everything from good to evil. Total game-changer."
[EN4 None] § The Big Mistake
[EN5 6] The woman looked at the tree and saw that the fruit looked delicious. Plus, the idea of knowing everything was super tempting. So she took a bite and gave some to her husband who was with her, and he ate it too.
[EN6 7] Instantly, it was like a filter came off. They saw everything, including the fact that they were totally naked! They scrambled to sew some fig leaves together for some makeshift clothes.
[EN7 8] Later, they heard God strolling through the garden in the cool evening breeze, and they freaked out and hid in the trees.
[EN8 9] God called out, "Where are you?"
[EN9 10] The man answered, "I heard you in the garden, and I was scared because I was naked, so I hid."
[EN10 11] God asked, "Who told you that you were naked? Did you eat from that tree I specifically told you not to eat from?"
[EN11 12] The man said, "The woman you gave me, she gave me the fruit, and yeah, I ate it."
[EN12 13] Then God said to the woman, "What have you done?"
[EN13 13] She said, "The snake tricked me, and I ate it."
[EN14 None] § The Consequences
[EN15 14-15] God said to the snake, "Because you did this, you're cursed. More cursed than any other animal. You'll crawl on your belly and eat dirt for the rest of your life. I'm putting a huge beef between you and the woman, and between your kids and her kids. He'll crush your head, and you'll strike his heel."
[EN16 16] To the woman, he said, "Giving birth is going to be super painful. You'll have your babies in pain. You'll want to be close to your husband, but he'll be the boss of you."
[EN17 17-19] To the man, he said, "Because you listened to your wife and ate from the tree I commanded you not to, the ground is now cursed because of you. Getting food from it will be a huge struggle for the rest of your life. It'll grow thorns and weeds, and you'll have to sweat and work your butt off just to eat, until you die and go back to the ground you came from. You started as dust, and you'll end up as dust."
[EN18 None] § Kicked Out
[EN19 20] The man, Adam, named his wife Eve, because she would be the mother of everyone.
[EN20 21] And God made them some better clothes out of leather and dressed them.
[EN21 22] Then God said, "Wow, the man has become like one of us, knowing good and evil. We can't let him reach out and eat from the Tree-of-Life and live forever. That can't happen."
[EN22 23-24] So God kicked them out of the Garden of Eden. He sent them out to farm the land they were made from. He threw them out and put powerful angels and a flaming, spinning sword to the east of the garden to guard the way to the Tree-of-Life.
