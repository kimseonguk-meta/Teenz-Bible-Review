===== CHAPTER 21: A Promise Kept, A Family Divided (21 EN, 21 KO, 18 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § The Promised Son
[EN1 1-5] God showed up for Sarah, just like he promised. And just like he said, Sarah got pregnant and had a son for Abraham in his old age. It happened at the exact time God had scheduled. Abraham named him Isaac, which means "laughter." When Isaac was eight days old, Abraham had him circumcised, following God's instructions. Abraham was 100 years old when Isaac was born.
[EN2 6-7] Sarah was so happy, she said, "God has made me laugh, and everyone who hears about this will laugh with me! Who would've ever guessed that I'd be nursing a baby at my age? But here I am, I've given my old husband a son!"
[EN3 8] The baby grew up and it was time to stop breastfeeding. Abraham threw a huge party to celebrate.
[EN4 None] § Hagar and Ishmael Sent Away
[EN5 9] But one day, Sarah saw Hagar's son, Ishmael, making fun of her son, Isaac. She got super mad and told Abraham, "You need to get rid of that slave woman and her son. Her son is not going to get any of my son's inheritance!"
[EN6 10-13] This was really tough for Abraham to hear. After all, Ishmael was his son too. But God told him, "Don't worry about the boy or Hagar. Just do what Sarah says. Your family line will continue through Isaac. And don't worry about Ishmael, I'll make a great nation out of him too, because he's your son."
[EN7 14-16] So, the next morning, Abraham got up early, packed some food and water for Hagar, and sent her and the boy away. She wandered into the Beersheba desert. When the water ran out, she put the boy under a bush and walked about 50 yards away. She couldn't bear to watch him die, so she just sat there and cried her eyes out.
[EN8 17-18] But God heard the boy crying. An angel called down from heaven, "What's wrong, Hagar? Don't be scared. God heard the boy and knows he's in a tough spot. Get up, go get your son, and hold him tight. I'm going to make him into a great nation."
[EN9 19] Just then, God opened her eyes and she saw a well full of water. She ran to it, filled up her canteen, and gave the boy a long, cool drink.
[EN10 20-21] God was with Ishmael as he grew up. He lived in the desert and became a pro archer. He settled in the Paran wilderness, and his mom found him a wife from Egypt.
[EN11 None] § A Treaty at Beersheba
[EN12 22-23] Around that same time, a king named Abimelech and his army commander, Phicol, came to Abraham and said, "Dude, it's obvious that God is with you in everything you do. So, promise me that you won't ever try to trick me or my family. As long as you're living in my land, you have to treat me and my people as well as I've treated you."
[EN13 24] Abraham replied, "You got it. I swear."
[EN14 25] But then Abraham brought up an issue. "Your servants stole a well from me," he told Abimelech.
[EN15 26] Abimelech was like, "I had no idea who did that. You never said anything to me about it. This is the first I'm hearing of it."
[EN16 27-28] So, they made a deal. Abraham gave Abimelech some sheep and cattle. Then, Abraham set aside seven more sheep.
[EN17 29] Abimelech asked, "What's with the seven extra sheep?"
[EN18 30] Abraham explained, "When you accept these seven sheep, it's proof that I'm the one who dug this well, and that it belongs to me."
[EN19 31-32] That's why they named the place Beersheba, which means "Oath-Well," because they both made a promise there. After they made their deal, Abimelech and his commander Phicol went back to their own territory.
[EN20 33-34] Abraham planted a special tree in Beersheba and prayed to God, the Eternal God. And Abraham lived in that Philistine area for a long, long time.
