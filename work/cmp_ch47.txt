===== CHAPTER 47: Joseph's Family Moves to Egypt (25 EN, 25 KO, 18 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § Family Reunion in Egypt
[EN1 1] Joseph went straight to Pharaoh and was like, “My dad and brothers are here from Canaan! They brought all their animals and everything they own. They’re hanging out in Goshen for now.”
[EN2 2-3] He brought five of his brothers to meet Pharaoh. Pharaoh asked them, “So, what do you guys do?”
[EN3 3-4] They replied, “We’re shepherds, just like our ancestors. We came here because the famine in Canaan is insane, and there’s no grass for our flocks. If it’s cool with you, we’d love to settle in Goshen.”
[EN4 5-6] Pharaoh looked at Joseph and said, “Awesome, your family’s here! Egypt is totally open to them. Let them live in Goshen, the best land we have. And hey, if any of them are really good at their job, put them in charge of my own livestock.”
[EN5 7-8] Then, Joseph brought his dad, Jacob, to meet Pharaoh. Jacob gave Pharaoh a blessing, and Pharaoh asked him, “Dang, how old are you?”
[EN6 9-10] Jacob said, “I’ve been wandering for 130 years. It’s been a tough life, and way shorter than my ancestors’ lives.” Then he blessed Pharaoh again and left.
[EN7 11-12] Joseph got his family all set up in Egypt. They got the best land in the region of Rameses, just like Pharaoh ordered. Joseph made sure everyone, from his dad to the youngest kid, had everything they needed.
[EN8 None] § The Famine Gets Real
[EN9 13-14] Eventually, the food ran out everywhere. The famine was no joke, and both Egypt and Canaan were struggling hard. Joseph collected all the money from people buying food and put it in Pharaoh’s treasury.
[EN10 15] When everyone’s money was gone, the Egyptians came to Joseph, begging, “We need food! Are you just gonna let us die? We’re totally broke.”
[EN11 16-17] Joseph was like, “Okay, if you’re out of cash, bring me your livestock. I’ll trade you food for them.” So, people brought their horses, sheep, cattle, and donkeys, and Joseph gave them food to get through the year.
[EN12 None] § Land for Food
[EN13 18-19] The next year, they were back. “Sir, you know we’re broke. Our money is gone, and you have our animals. The only things we have left are our bodies and our land. Seriously, are you gonna watch us starve? Let’s make a deal. Take us and our land in exchange for food. We’ll be Pharaoh’s slaves, but at least give us some seeds so we can grow crops and not die.”
[EN14 20-21] So, Joseph bought all the land in Egypt for Pharaoh. Every single Egyptian sold their land because the famine was that bad. Pharaoh ended up owning all the land, and the people became his slaves.
[EN15 22] But Joseph didn’t buy the priests’ land. They got a regular allowance from Pharaoh, so they didn’t need to sell.
[EN16 23-24] Then Joseph told the people, “Alright, listen up. I’ve bought you and your land for Pharaoh. Now, I’m giving you seeds to plant. When you harvest the crops, one-fifth of it goes to Pharaoh. You can keep the other four-fifths for yourselves, for seeds and for feeding your families.”
[EN17 25] They were all like, “You’re a lifesaver! We’re actually happy to be Pharaoh’s slaves.”
[EN18 26] Joseph made it a law in Egypt that a fifth of all produce belongs to Pharaoh. This law is still around today. Only the priests’ land wasn’t owned by Pharaoh.
[EN19 None] § Jacob's Final Request
[EN20 27-28] So the Israelites settled in Goshen, got some property, and their population exploded. Jacob lived in Egypt for 17 years, making his grand total 147 years old.
[EN21 29] When Jacob knew he was about to die, he called for Joseph. “Do me a solid. Swear to me you’ll be loyal and won’t bury me in Egypt. When I die, I want you to carry me out of Egypt and bury me with my ancestors.”
[EN22 30] Joseph said, “I got you. I’ll do it.”
[EN23 31] “Swear to me,” Jacob insisted. And Joseph swore.
[EN24 31] Then, from his bed, Jacob bowed his head, grateful and at peace.
