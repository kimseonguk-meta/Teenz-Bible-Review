===== CHAPTER 11: God Mixes Up Languages at Babel Tower (20 EN, 20 KO, 19 MSG) =====
[EN0 1-4] Back in the day, everyone on Earth spoke the same language. As people moved eastward, they found a nice plain in Shinar and decided to settle there. They were like, "Yo, let's make some bricks and bake 'em real good." They used these bricks instead of stone and tar for their glue.
[EN1 4] Then they got this big idea: "Let's build a massive city with a tower that scrapes the sky! We'll make a name for ourselves, so we don't get scattered all over the place."
[EN2 5-7] So God came down to check out the city and the tower they were building. He saw what was up and said, "Look at this. One group of people, one language. This is just the beginning. Who knows what they'll try next? They'll think they can do anything. Nah, I'm going down there to mix up their language so they can't understand each other."
[EN3 8-9] And just like that, God scattered them all over the world, and they had to stop building the city. That's why it's called Babel, because God turned their language into a bunch of babble. From there, he spread them out across the globe.
[EN4 None] § Shem's Family Line
[EN5 10-11] Here's the deal with Shem's family. When Shem was 100 years old, he had a son named Arphaxad. This was two years after the great flood. After Arphaxad was born, Shem lived for another 500 years and had more kids.
[EN6 12-13] When Arphaxad was 35, he had Shelah. Then he lived for another 403 years and had more kids.
[EN7 14-15] When Shelah was 30, he had Eber. Then he lived for another 403 years and had more kids.
[EN8 16-17] When Eber was 34, he had Peleg. Then he lived for another 430 years and had more kids.
[EN9 18-19] When Peleg was 30, he had Reu. Then he lived for another 209 years and had more kids.
[EN10 20-21] When Reu was 32, he had Serug. Then he lived for another 207 years and had more kids.
[EN11 22-23] When Serug was 30, he had Nahor. Then he lived for another 200 years and had more kids.
[EN12 24-25] When Nahor was 29, he had Terah. Then he lived for another 119 years and had more kids.
[EN13 26] When Terah was 70, he had Abram, Nahor, and Haran.
[EN14 None] § Terah's Family
[EN15 27-28] So here's Terah's story. He was the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran. Haran had a son named Lot. Haran actually died in his hometown, Ur of the Chaldees, before his own dad, Terah, passed away.
[EN16 29] Abram and Nahor both got married. Abram's wife was Sarai, and Nahor's wife was Milcah. Milcah was the daughter of his brother Haran, who also had another daughter named Iscah.
[EN17 30] Here's the sad part: Sarai couldn't have kids. She was barren.
[EN18 31] One day, Terah took his son Abram, his grandson Lot (Haran's son), and his daughter-in-law Sarai (Abram's wife) and they all left Ur of the Chaldees to go to the land of Canaan. But when they reached a place called Haran, they decided to just stay there.
[EN19 32] Terah lived to be 205 years old and died in Haran.
