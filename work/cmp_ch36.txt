===== CHAPTER 36: Esau's Family Tree: The Whole Squad (25 EN, 25 KO, 24 MSG) =====
[EN0 1] Here’s the rundown on Esau's family, also known as Edom.
[EN1 None] § Esau's Wives and Kids
[EN2 2-5] First off, Esau married some local girls from Canaan. There was Adah, the daughter of Elon the Hittite; Oholibamah, who was the daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon the Hivite; and Basemath, Ishmael’s daughter and sister of Nebaioth.
[EN3 4-5] Adah had a son named Eliphaz. Basemath had Reuel. And Oholibamah had Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. These were Esau's sons, all born in Canaan.
[EN4 6-8] Later on, Esau packed up everything—his wives, kids, all the people in his household, his livestock, and all the stuff he had gathered in Canaan—and moved a good distance away from his brother Jacob. They just had way too much stuff to live in the same area, and the land couldn't support all their animals. So, Esau ended up settling in the hill country of Seir. Just so you know, Esau and Edom are the same guy.
[EN5 None] § Esau's Descendants, The Edomites
[EN6 9-10] So, this is the official family tree of Esau, the guy who started the Edomite nation in the hill country of Seir. The names of Esau’s sons were Eliphaz (from his wife Adah) and Reuel (from his wife Basemath).
[EN7 11-12] Eliphaz’s sons were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz. Eliphaz also had a son named Amalek with his concubine Timna. These were all grandsons of Esau through his wife Adah.
[EN8 13] Reuel’s sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. These were the grandsons of Esau through his wife Basemath.
[EN9 14] Esau’s wife Oholibamah, the daughter of Anah (who was the son of Zibeon), gave him three sons: Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.
[EN10 None] § The Chieftains from Esau
[EN11 15-18] These are the leaders, or chieftains, that came from Esau's family. From Eliphaz, Esau's firstborn, came the chieftains Teman, Omar, Zepho, Kenaz, Korah, Gatam, and Amalek. These were all leaders in the land of Edom, all descended from Adah.
[EN12 17] From Reuel, Esau's son, came the chieftains Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. These guys were the leaders from Reuel's line in Edom, all descended from Basemath.
[EN13 18] And from Esau's wife Oholibamah came the chieftains Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.
[EN14 19] So yeah, these are the sons of Esau (aka Edom) and their main leaders.
[EN15 None] § The Original People of Seir
[EN16 20-21] Now, here's the family tree of Seir the Horite, the people who were living in that land originally: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These were the chieftains of the Horites in the land of Edom.
[EN17 22-26] Lotan’s sons were Hori and Homam, and his sister was Timna. Shobal’s sons were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam. Zibeon’s sons were Aiah and Anah. This is the same Anah who randomly found hot springs in the desert while he was out watching his dad's donkeys. Pretty cool find.
[EN18 27-30] The children of Anah were Dishon and his daughter Oholibamah. The sons of Dishon were Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Keran. The sons of Ezer were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan. The sons of Dishan were Uz and Aran.
[EN19 29-30] These were the original Horite chieftains, clan by clan, in the land of Seir.

[EN21 31-39] Check it out, these are the kings who ruled in Edom way before Israel even had a king. First up was King Bela, son of Beor, from the city of Dinhabah. [EN23 40-42] And finally, here's the full list of chieftains from Esau's line, sorted by their clans and regions: Timna, Alvah, Jetheth, Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, Magdiel, and Iram. These were all the chieftains of Edom in their territories.
