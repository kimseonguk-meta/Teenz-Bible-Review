===== CHAPTER 10: Noah's Kids and Their Family Trees (16 EN paras, 17 MSG paras) =====
[EN0 None] § Noah's Family Tree
[EN1 1] Alright, so here's the rundown of Noah's sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. After the big flood, they started having their own kids.
[EN2 2-5] Japheth's sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. Gomer's sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. Javan's sons were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Rodanim. All the people who lived by the sea came from these guys, each group in their own spot, with their own families and languages.
[EN3 None] § Ham's Line
[EN4 6-7] Ham's sons were Cush, Egypt, Put, and Canaan. Cush's sons were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. Raamah's sons were Sheba and Dedan.
[EN5 8-12] Cush also had a son named Nimrod. This dude was the first absolute legend warrior on the planet. He was an insane hunter, so good that people would say, "You're a beast hunter, just like Nimrod!" His kingdom started with Babel, and then he took over Erech, Akkad, and Calneh in Shinar. From there, he went up to Asshur and built the cities of Nineveh, Rehoboth Ir, Calah, and Resen, which was between Nineveh and the mega-city Calah.
[EN6 13-14] Egypt was the ancestor of the Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, Naphtuhim, Pathrusim, Casluhim (where the Philistines came from), and the Kaphtorim.
[EN7 15-19] Canaan’s first son was Sidon, and then he had Heth. From him came the Jebusites, Amorites, Girgashites, Hivites, Arkites, Sinites, Arvadites, Zemarites, and Hamathites. Later on, the Canaanite people spread out from Sidon all the way down to Gerar and Gaza, and then east to Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboiim, and Lasha.
[EN8 20] So yeah, that's the whole family line of Ham, sorted by family, language, country, and nation.
[EN9 None] § Shem's Line
[EN10 21] Shem, who was Japheth's older brother, had kids too. He was the ancestor of all of Eber's descendants.
[EN11 22-23] Shem's sons were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram. Aram's sons were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech.
[EN12 24-25] Arphaxad had Shelah, and Shelah had Eber. Eber had two sons. The first was named Peleg, which means 'division', because during his time, everyone on Earth got split up. His other son was Joktan.
[EN13 26-30] Joktan had a bunch of sons: Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, Obal, Abimael, Sheba, Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. Their territory stretched from Mesha all the way to the eastern mountains of Sephar.
[EN14 31] So that's the family line of Shem, all sorted by family, language, country, and nation.
[EN15 32] This is the complete family tree of Noah's sons, and how they grew into different nations. From these guys, nations spread out all over the world after the flood.
