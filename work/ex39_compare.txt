--- MSG [2-5] ---
Ephod. They made the Ephod using gold and blue, purple, and scarlet fabrics and finely twisted linen. They hammered out gold leaf and sliced it into threads that were then worked into designs in the blue, purple, and scarlet fabric and fine linen. They made shoulder pieces fastened at the two ends. The decorated band was made of the same material—gold, blue, purple, and scarlet material, and of fine twisted linen—and of one piece with it, just as God had commanded Moses.

>>> TEEN:

They literally hammered out gold into thin sheets, cut it into threads, and wove it right into the fabric. It had these shoulder straps that attached at the ends, and the fancy waistband was all one piece with the rest of it, made from the same awesome materials. All of it, just how God laid it out for Moses.
  [KO p1] 에봇. 다음은 에봇을 만들었는데, 금실이랑 청색, 자색, 홍색 실, 그리고 가늘게 꼰 베실을 썼대. 금을 얇게 펴서 실처럼 잘라가지고 청색, 자색, 홍색 실이랑 고운 베실에 섞어서 디자인을 넣은 거임. 양쪽 끝에 다는 어깨받이도 만들었고, 허리띠도 같은 재료로, 즉 금실, 청색, 자색, 홍색 실, 그리고 가늘게 꼰 베실로 에봇에 딱 붙여서 하나처럼 만들었어. 이것도 하나님이 모세한테 시킨 그대로.
