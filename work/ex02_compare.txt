--- MSG [1-3] ---
A man from the family of Levi married a Levite woman. The woman became pregnant and had a son. She saw there was something special about him and hid him. She hid him for three months. When she couldn’t hide him any longer she got a little basket-boat made of papyrus, waterproofed it with tar and pitch, and placed the child in it. Then she set it afloat in the reeds at the edge of the Nile.

>>> TEEN:
  [p0 badge=1-2] So, check it out. This guy from the Levi family married a woman from the same tribe. She got pregnant and had a son. Right away, she could tell there was something super special about him, so she hid him for three months.
  [KO p0] 레위 집안의 어떤 남자가 같은 레위 지파 여자랑 결혼했대. 그리고 그 여자가 임신해서 아들을 낳은 거임. 딱 보니까 애가 뭔가 보통이 아닌 거야, 완전 특별해 보였대. 그래서 석 달 동안 아기를 몰래 숨겨서 키웠어.
  [p1 badge=3-4] When she just couldn't keep him a secret anymore, she got a small papyrus basket, basically a little boat, and waterproofed it with tar. She put the baby inside and set it floating in the reeds along the edge of the Nile River.
  [KO p1] 근데 더 이상은 숨길 수가 없게 된 거지. 그래서 갈대로 만든 작은 상자, 거의 뭐 보트 같은 걸 구해서 역청이랑 나무 진으로 방수 처리를 싹 한 거야. 그리고 아기를 그 안에 넣어서 나일강 가장자리 갈대 사이에 띄워 보냈대.

--- MSG [4-6] ---
The baby’s older sister found herself a vantage point a little way off and watched to see what would happen to him. Pharaoh’s daughter came down to the Nile to bathe; her maidens strolled on the bank. She saw the basket-boat floating in the reeds and sent her maid to get it. She opened it and saw the child—a baby crying! Her heart went out to him. She said, “This must be one of the Hebrew babies.”

>>> TEEN:
  [p1 badge=3-4] When she just couldn't keep him a secret anymore, she got a small papyrus basket, basically a little boat, and waterproofed it with tar. She put the baby inside and set it floating in the reeds along the edge of the Nile River.
  [KO p1] 근데 더 이상은 숨길 수가 없게 된 거지. 그래서 갈대로 만든 작은 상자, 거의 뭐 보트 같은 걸 구해서 역청이랑 나무 진으로 방수 처리를 싹 한 거야. 그리고 아기를 그 안에 넣어서 나일강 가장자리 갈대 사이에 띄워 보냈대.
  [p2 badge=4] The baby's big sister was watching from a distance to see what would go down.

She opened it up and saw the baby—and he was crying! Her heart totally melted. She was like, "This has to be one of the Hebrew babies."
  [KO p3] 그때 마침 파라오 딸이 목욕하러 나일강에 내려왔는데, 시녀들은 강둑을 거닐고 있었지. 공주가 갈대 사이에 있는 상자를 본 거야. 그래서 시녀를 시켜서 가져오게 했어. 열어보니까 아기가 떡하니 있는데, 막 울고 있는 거임! 공주 마음이 완전 약해졌지. "이거 분명 히브리 사람들 아기일 거야"라고 말했대.

--- MSG [11-12] ---
