===== CHAPTER 15: God's Epic Promise to Abram (14 EN, 14 KO, 11 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § God's Covenant Promise
[EN1 1] After all this went down, God's word came to Abram in a vision, like a super vivid dream. 'Don't be scared, Abram. I've got your back. Your reward is gonna be huge!'
[EN2 2-3] But Abram was like, 'God, my King, what good are your gifts if I don't have any kids? My servant Eliezer from Damascus is gonna get everything.' Abram went on, 'Look, you haven't given me any children, so now some guy who works for me is gonna inherit it all.'
[EN3 4] Then God's message came through loud and clear: 'Chill, he's not your heir. A son from your own body will be your heir.'
[EN4 5] Then God took him outside and said, 'Look up at the sky. Try to count the stars. Can you even? That's how many descendants you're gonna have! You're going to have a massive family, Abram!'
[EN5 6] And Abram actually believed it! He trusted God! And because of that, God said he was 'all good with God.'
[EN6 None] § A Weird Ceremony
[EN7 7] God kept going, 'I'm the same God who brought you from Ur of the Chaldees to give you this land to own.'
[EN8 8] Abram asked, 'My King, God, how can I be sure it'll all be mine?'
[EN9 9-10] God said, 'Bring me a three-year-old heifer, a three-year-old goat, a three-year-old ram, a dove, and a young pigeon.'
[EN10 10-12] So Abram brought all the animals, cut them in half, and laid the pieces across from each other. But he didn't cut the birds. Vultures started swooping down to eat the carcasses, but Abram shooed them away. As the sun started to set, Abram fell into a super deep sleep, and then this creepy, dark, and heavy feeling of dread washed over him.
[EN11 None] § A Glimpse of the Future
[EN12 13-16] God told Abram, 'Listen up: your descendants will be foreigners in a land that's not theirs. They'll be slaves and get treated horribly for 400 years. But then I'll bring the hammer down on the nation they serve, and your kids will march out of there with tons of loot. But not you. You'll live a long, full life and die peacefully. It won't be until the fourth generation that your descendants come back here, because the Amorites are still deep in their sin.'
[EN13 17-21] When the sun was totally gone and it was pitch black, a smoking firepot and a blazing torch passed between the animal halves. That's when God made a solid promise with Abram: 'I'm giving this land to your descendants, from the Nile River in Egypt all the way to the Euphrates River in Assyria—the land of the Kenites, Kenizzites, Kadmonites, Hittites, Perizzites, Rephaim, Amorites, Canaanites, Girgashites, and Jebusites.'

[MSG0 15] After all these things, this word of God came to Abram in a vision: “Don’t be afraid, Abram. I’m your shield. Your reward will be grand!”
[MSG1 2-3] Abram said, “God, Master, what use are your gifts as long as I’m childless and Eliezer of Damascus is going to inherit everything?” Abram continued, “See, you’ve given me no children, and now a mere house servant is going to get it all.”
[MSG2 4] Then God’s Message came: “Don’t worry, he won’t be your heir; a son from your body will be your heir.”
[MSG3 5] Then he took him outside and said, “Look at the sky. Count the stars. Can you do it? Count your descendants! You’re going to have a big family, Abram!”
[MSG4 6] And he believed! Believed God! God declared him “Set-Right-with-God.”
[MSG5 7] God continued, “I’m the same God who brought you from Ur of the Chaldees and gave you this land to own.”
[MSG6 8] Abram said, “Master God, how am I to know this, that it will all be mine?”
[MSG7 9] God said, “Bring me a heifer, a goat, and a ram, each three years old, and a dove and a young pigeon.”
[MSG8 10-12] He brought all these animals to him, split them down the middle, and laid the halves opposite each other. But he didn’t split the birds. Vultures swooped down on the carcasses, but Abram scared them off. As the sun went down a deep sleep overcame Abram and then a sense of dread, dark and heavy.
[MSG9 13-16] God said to Abram, “Know this: your descendants will live as outsiders in a land not theirs; they’ll be enslaved and beaten down for 400 years. Then I’ll punish their slave masters; your offspring will march out of there loaded with plunder. But not you; you’ll have a long and full life and die a good and peaceful death. Not until the fourth generation will your descendants return here; sin is still a thriving business among the Amorites.”
[MSG10 17-21] When the sun was down and it was dark, a smoking firepot and a flaming torch moved between the split carcasses. That’s when God made a covenant with Abram: “I’m giving this land to your children, from the Nile River in Egypt to the River Euphrates in Assyria—the country of the Kenites, Kenizzites, Kadmonites, Hittites, Perizzites, Rephaim, Amorites, Canaanites, Girgashites, and Jebusites.”
