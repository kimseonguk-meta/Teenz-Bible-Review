[EN1 None] § The Flood Begins
[EN2 6-10] Noah was 600 years old when the flood hit. He, his wife, his sons, and their wives all got on the ark to stay safe from the floodwaters. All the animals—clean and unclean, birds, and all the little crawly things—came to Noah and boarded the ark in pairs, a male and a female, just like God had ordered. Seven days later, the flood started for real.
[EN3 11-12] On the 17th day of the second month of Noah’s 600th year, everything went down. All the underground water sources exploded, and it was like the sky’s windows were thrown wide open. The rain was insane, pouring down for 40 days and 40 nights without stopping.
[EN4 13-16] On that exact day, Noah and his family—his sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth, his wife, and his sons' wives—all got on the ark. They brought every kind of animal with them: wild animals, farm animals, all the creatures that crawl on the ground, and every kind of bird. Every living thing that breathes came to Noah and got on the ark in pairs. A male and a female of every species came, just as God had commanded Noah. Then God himself shut the door behind them.
[EN5 None] § Total Wipeout
[EN6 17-23] The flood kept going for 40 days. The water level just kept rising, lifting the ark way up high above the ground. The water got deeper and deeper until the ark was just floating on the surface. It got so bad that even the tallest mountains were completely underwater—the water was like 20 feet above the highest peaks. Everything died. Every single living thing that moved on the earth was gone. Birds, farm animals, wild animals, all the swarming creatures—gone. And all the people—gone too. Every living, breathing thing on dry land died.
[EN7 23-24] God wiped out everything and everyone—people, animals, crawling things, and birds. They were all erased from the earth. The only ones who survived were Noah and everyone who was with him on the ark. The water covered everything for 150 days.
