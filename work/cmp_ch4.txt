===== CHAPTER 4: The First Murder and Its Epic Consequences =====
[EN0 1-2] So, Adam and Eve got together, and soon enough, Eve was pregnant and had a baby boy named Cain. She was like, 'OMG, I made a person, with God's help!' After that, she had another son, Abel. Abel grew up to be a shepherd, taking care of sheep, while Cain was a farmer.
[EN1 None] § The First Sibling Rivalry
[EN2 3-5] After a while, it was time to give offerings to God. Cain, being a farmer, brought some of his crops. Abel brought the best cuts of meat from his firstborn lambs. God was really happy with Abel's gift, but not so much with Cain's. Cain got super mad and threw a major fit.
[EN3 6-7] God asked Cain, 'Yo, what's with the attitude? Why the long face? If you do the right thing, you'll be fine. But if you don't, sin is like a predator waiting to jump you. It's after you, but you have to be the one in control.'
[EN4 8] Cain talked to his brother Abel. While they were out in the field, Cain attacked Abel and killed him.
[EN5 None] § The Cover-Up Fail
[EN6 9] God asked Cain, 'Where's your brother Abel?'
[EN7 9] He shot back, 'How am I supposed to know? Am I my brother's babysitter or something?'
[EN8 10-12] God said, 'What did you do?! Your brother's blood is screaming to me from the ground. And now, you're cursed. The ground that soaked up your brother's blood won't grow anything for you anymore. You're going to be a homeless wanderer on this planet.'
[EN9 13-14] Cain told God, 'This is too much! I can't handle this punishment. You're kicking me off my land, and I can't even face you. I'll be a homeless wanderer, and anyone who finds me will probably kill me.'
[EN10 15] But God told him, 'Nah. Anyone who kills you will get it back seven times worse.' Then God put a special mark on Cain so that no one would kill him on sight.
[EN11 16] So Cain left God's presence and went to live in the Land of Nod, which is east of Eden.
[EN12 None] § Cain's Family Line
[EN13 17-18] Cain and his wife had a kid named Enoch. Cain then built a city and named it Enoch after his son. Enoch had a son named Irad, Irad had Mehujael, Mehujael had Methushael, and Methushael had Lamech.
[EN14 19-22] Lamech married two women, Adah and Zillah. Adah had a son named Jabal, who was the OG of everyone who lives in tents and raises livestock. His brother was Jubal, the ancestor of all musicians who play the harp and flute. Zillah had a son named Tubal-Cain, who was a master blacksmith, making all sorts of bronze and iron tools. His sister was Naamah.
[EN15 23-24] Lamech said to his wives, 'Adah and Zillah, listen up! I killed a guy for hurting me, a young dude who attacked me. If Cain gets avenged seven times, then for me, it's seventy-seven times!'
[EN16 None] § A New Hope
[EN17 25-26] Adam and Eve got together again, and she had another son named Seth. She said, 'God has given me another child to replace Abel, since Cain killed him.' Seth also had a son, and he named him Enosh. It was around this time that people started to seriously pray and worship God.
