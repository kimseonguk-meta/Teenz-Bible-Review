--- MSG [1-9] ---
God spoke to Moses: “Tell the Israelites that they are to set aside offerings for me. Receive the offerings from everyone who is willing to give. These are the offerings I want you to receive from them: gold, silver, bronze; blue, purple, and scarlet material; fine linen; goats’ hair; tanned rams’ skins; dolphin skins; acacia wood; lamp oil; spices for anointing oils and for fragrant incense; onyx stones and other stones for setting in the Ephod and the Breastpiece. Let them construct a Sanctuary for me so that I can live among them. You are to construct it following the plans I’ve given you, the design for The Dwelling and the design for all its furnishings.

>>> TEEN:
  [p0 badge=1-9] God told Moses, 'Yo, tell the Israelites it's time to bring some offerings for me. But only from the people who are actually down to give. Here's the list of stuff I'm looking for: gold, silver, bronze, some cool blue, purple, and red fabrics, nice linen, goat hair, tanned ram skins, dolphin skins, acacia wood, lamp oil, spices for anointing oil and for some sweet-smelling incense, onyx stones, and other gems to put in the Ephod and the Breastpiece. I want them to build a Sanctuary for me, a place where I can come and hang out with them. Make sure you build it exactly like the plans I'm giving you, for the whole setup and all its furniture.'
  [KO p0] 하나님이 모세한테 말했대. "이스라엘 백성들한테 나를 위해 예물을 가져오라고 전해라. 기쁜 마음으로 내는 사람한테서만 예물을 받아. 내가 받고 싶은 예물은 이런 것들이야: 금, 은, 청동, 파란색, 보라색, 빨간색 실, 고운 아마포, 염소 털, 붉게 물들인 숫양 가죽, 돌고래 가죽, 아카시아 나무, 등잔용 기름, 성소에 바르는 기름과 향기로운 향을 만드는 데 쓰는 향료, 에봇과 가슴받이에 박을 호마노와 다른 보석들. 이걸로 내가 그들 가운데 살 수 있도록 성소를 만들게 해. 내가 너에게 보여주는 설계도대로, 성막과 그 안의 모든 기구를 만들어야 한다."

--- MSG [10-15] ---
“First let them make a Chest using acacia wood: make it three and three-quarters feet long and two and one-quarter feet wide and deep. Cover it with a veneer of pure gold inside and out and make a molding of gold all around it. Cast four gold rings and attach them to its four feet, two rings on one side and two rings on the other. Make poles from acacia wood and cover them with a veneer of gold and insert them into the rings on the sides of the Chest for carrying the Chest. The poles are to stay in the rings; they must not be removed.

>>> TEEN:
  [p1 badge=10-15] Alright, first up, have them build a Chest out of acacia wood. It should be 3.75 feet long and 2.25 feet wide and deep. Slap a layer of pure gold on it, inside and out, and add a gold trim all the way around. Make four gold rings and stick them on the four corners, two on each side. Then, make some poles from acacia wood, cover them in gold too, and slide them into the rings on the sides of the Chest so you can carry it. And for real, leave the poles in the rings. Don't take them out.
  [KO p1] "먼저 아카시아 나무로 궤를 만들게 해. 길이는 1.7미터, 너비와 높이는 각각 1미터 정도로. 안팎을 순금으로 싹 다 감싸고, 위쪽 가장자리에는 금테를 둘러. 금 고리 네 개를 만들어서 네 발에 달아. 한쪽에 두 개, 다른 쪽에 두 개씩. 그리고 아카시아 나무로 채를 만들어서 금으로 싼 다음에, 궤를 운반할 때 쓰도록 양쪽 고리에 끼워. 이 채는 궤의 고리에 그대로 둬야 해. 절대 빼면 안 돼."

--- MSG [18-22] ---
“Sculpt two winged angels out of hammered gold for either end of the Atonement-Cover, one angel at one end, one angel at the other. Make them of one piece with the Atonement-Cover. Make the angels with their wings spread, hovering over the Atonement-Cover, facing one another but looking down on it. Set the Atonement-Cover as a lid over the Chest and place in the Chest The Testimony that I will give you. I will meet you there at set times and speak with you from above the Atonement-Cover and from between the angel-figures that are on it, speaking the commands that I have for the Israelites.

>>> TEEN:
  [p4 badge=18-22] Then, sculpt two winged angels out of hammered gold for each end of the Atonement-Cover, one on each side. They should be all one piece with the cover. The angels should have their wings spread out, covering the Atonement-Cover, and they should be facing each other but looking down at it. Put this Atonement-Cover on top of the Chest, and inside the Chest, put the Testimony I'm gonna give you. I'll meet you there at specific times and talk to you from above the Atonement-Cover, from between the two angel statues on it. I'll give you all the commands for the Israelites from there.
  [KO p4] "망치로 두들겨 편 금으로 날개 달린 천사, 즉 그룹 둘을 속죄소 양쪽 끝에 만들어. 한쪽 끝에 하나, 다른 쪽 끝에 하나씩. 속죄소와 한 덩어리가 되게 만들어야 해. 그룹들은 날개를 위로 펴서 속죄소를 덮고, 서로 얼굴을 마주보되 속죄소를 내려다보는 자세로. 이 속죄소를 궤 위에 뚜껑처럼 덮고, 궤 안에는 내가 너에게 줄 증거판을 넣어둬. 내가 정한 때에 거기서 너를 만나고, 속죄소 위, 그 위에 있는 두 그룹 사이에서 너에게 말할 거야. 이스라엘 백성들에게 전할 내 모든 명령을 거기서 말해줄게."

--- MSG [23-28] ---
“Next make a Table from acacia wood. Make it three feet long, one and one-half feet wide and two and one-quarter feet high. Cover it with a veneer of pure gold. Make a molding all around it of gold. Make the border a handbreadth wide all around it and a rim of gold for the border. Make four rings of gold and attach the rings to the four legs parallel to the tabletop. They will serve as holders for the poles used to carry the Table. Make the poles of acacia wood and cover them with a veneer of gold. They will be used to carry the Table.

>>> TEEN:

  [KO p5] "다음은 아카시아 나무로 상을 만들어. 길이는 1.4미터, 너비는 0.7미터, 높이는 1미터로. 순금으로 감싸고 주위에 금테를 둘러. 상 가장자리에는 손바닥 너비만 한 턱을 만들고, 그 턱 주위에도 금테를 둘러. 금 고리 네 개를 만들어서 네 다리 위쪽, 상판 바로 아래에 달아. 이 고리들은 상을 운반하는 채를 끼우는 곳이야. 아카시아 나무로 채를 만들어서 금으로 싸. 이걸로 상을 운반할 거야."

--- MSG [31-36] ---
“Make a Lampstand of pure hammered gold. Make its stem and branches, cups, calyxes, and petals all of one piece. Give it six branches, three from one side and three from the other; put three cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with calyx and petals, on one branch, three on the next, and so on—the same for all six branches. On the main stem of the Lampstand, make four cups shaped like almonds, with calyx and petals, a calyx extending from under each pair of the six branches, the entire Lampstand fashioned from one piece of hammered pure gold.
