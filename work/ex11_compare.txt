--- MSG [2-3] ---

--- MSG [4-7] ---
Then Moses confronted Pharaoh: “God’s Message: ‘At midnight I will go through Egypt and every firstborn child in Egypt will die, from the firstborn of Pharaoh, who sits on his throne, to the firstborn of the slave girl working at her hand mill. Also the firstborn of animals. Widespread wailing will erupt all over the country, lament such as has never been and never will be again. But against the Israelites—man, woman, or animal—there won’t be so much as a dog’s bark, so that you’ll know that God makes a clear distinction between Egypt and Israel.’

>>> TEEN:
  [p2 badge=4-7] Then Moses went right up to Pharaoh and was like, "Here's a message from God: 'Around midnight, I'm going to go through all of Egypt, and every firstborn son is going to die. From your son, Pharaoh, who's next in line for the throne, to the son of the servant girl grinding flour. Even the firstborn animals will die. There's going to be a massive outcry all over Egypt, like nothing you've ever heard before or will ever hear again. But for the Israelites? Not a single dog will bark at them. That's how you'll know God is making a serious distinction between Egypt and Israel.'"
  [KO p2] 그러고 나서 모세가 파라오한테 가서 말했어. "하나님 메시지임: '오늘 밤 자정에 내가 이집트를 싹 돌면서 이집트의 모든 처음 난 것들을 죽일 거다. 왕좌에 앉은 파라오의 맏아들부터 맷돌질하는 노예의 맏아들까지. 동물의 첫 녀석도 마찬가지임. 온 나라에 대성통곡이 터져 나올 건데, 전에도 없었고 앞으로도 없을 전설급 통곡일 거다. 근데 이스라엘 사람들한테는 남자든 여자든 동물이든 개 짖는 소리 하나 안 날 거임. 그래야 너희가 하나님이 이집트랑 이스라엘을 확실히 구분한다는 걸 알게 될 테니까.'"
