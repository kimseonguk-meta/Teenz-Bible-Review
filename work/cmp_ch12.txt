===== CHAPTER 12: Abram's Big Move and a Big Lie (10 EN, 10 KO, 11 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § God's Epic Promise
[EN1 1-3] God told Abram, "Yo, you gotta leave your country, your fam, and your dad's place for a land that I'm gonna show you. I'm gonna make you into a huge nation and bless you big time. I'll make you famous, and you'll be a major blessing to everyone. I'll have your back—anyone who blesses you, I'll bless them. Anyone who messes with you, I'll curse them. Seriously, every family on Earth is gonna be blessed because of you."
[EN2 None] § The Journey Begins
[EN3 4-6] So Abram just did what God said. He bounced, and his nephew Lot went with him. Get this—Abram was 75 years old when he left Haran. He took his wife Sarai, his nephew Lot, all their stuff, and all the people they had gathered in Haran, and they headed out for Canaan. And they made it, no problem.
[EN4 6-9] Abram traveled through the land all the way to a place called Shechem, by the big tree of Moreh. Back then, the Canaanites were living in that area. Then God showed up to Abram and said, "I'm going to give this land to your descendants." So Abram built an altar right there where God had appeared to him. After that, he moved on to the hill country east of Bethel and set up his camp. He built another altar there and prayed to God. Abram just kept on moving, heading south toward the Negev desert.
[EN5 None] § A Risky Plan in Egypt
[EN6 10-13] Then, a huge famine hit the land. It was so bad that Abram had to go down to Egypt to find food. As they were getting close to Egypt, he said to his wife Sarai, "Look, let's be real, you're crazy beautiful. When the Egyptians see you, they're gonna be like, 'Whoa, that's his wife!' and they'll totally kill me to get to you. But they'll let you live. So do me a solid: just tell them you're my sister. That way, they'll be cool with me and I won't get killed because of you."
[EN7 14-15] When Abram got to Egypt, the Egyptians took one look at his wife and were like, "OMG, she's absolutely stunning." The Pharaoh's officials were raving about her to him, and next thing you know, she was taken to live in Pharaoh's palace.
[EN8 16-17] Because of her, Abram was treated like an honored guest. He got loaded with sheep, cattle, donkeys, servants, and camels. But then God started hitting Pharaoh and his whole palace with some serious plagues, all because of Sarai, Abram's wife.
[EN9 18-20] Pharaoh called Abram and was like, "Dude, what have you done to me?! Why didn't you just tell me she was your wife? Why'd you say, 'She's my sister,' so I ended up taking her as my wife? Here, take your wife back—and get out!" Pharaoh then ordered his men to escort Abram, his wife, and all his stuff out of the country.

[MSG4 8] He moved on from there to the hill country east of Bethel and pitched his tent between Bethel to the west and Ai to the east. He built an altar there and prayed to God.
[MSG5 9] Abram kept moving, steadily making his way south, to the Negev.
[MSG6 10-13] Then a famine came to the land. Abram went down to Egypt to live; it was a hard famine. As he drew near to Egypt, he said to his wife, Sarai, “Look. We both know that you’re a beautiful woman. When the Egyptians see you they’re going to say, ‘Aha! That’s his wife!’ and kill me. But they’ll let you live. Do me a favor: tell them you’re my sister. Because of you, they’ll welcome me and let me live.”
[MSG7 14-15] When Abram arrived in Egypt, the Egyptians took one look and saw that his wife was stunningly beautiful. Pharaoh’s princes raved over her to Pharaoh. She was taken to live with Pharaoh.
[MSG8 16-17] Because of her, Abram got along very well: he accumulated sheep and cattle, male and female donkeys, men and women servants, and camels. But God hit Pharaoh hard because of Abram’s wife Sarai; everybody in the palace got seriously sick.
[MSG9 18-19] Pharaoh called for Abram, “What’s this that you’ve done to me? Why didn’t you tell me that she’s your wife? Why did you say, ‘She’s my sister’ so that I’d take her as my wife? Here’s your wife back—take her and get out!”
[MSG10 20] Pharaoh ordered his men to get Abram out of the country. They sent him and his wife and everything he owned on their way.
