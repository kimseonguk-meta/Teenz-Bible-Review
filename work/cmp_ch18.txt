===== CHAPTER 18: God's Surprise Visit and an Epic Bargain (32 EN, 32 KO, 21 MSG) =====
[EN0 None] § A Surprise Visit
[EN1 1-2] So, God showed up at Abraham's place by the big oak trees of Mamre. Abraham was just chilling at his tent's entrance, trying to escape the crazy heat of the day. He looked up and, boom, three guys were standing right there. He bolted from his tent to say what's up and bowed down to them.
[EN2 3-5] He was like, "Yo, Master, if you're cool with it, please hang out with me for a bit. I'll get some water so you can wash your feet. Just chill under this tree. I'll grab some food to get you energized for your trip, since you're passing by my spot anyway."
[EN3 5] They were like, "For sure. Go for it."
[EN4 6] Abraham rushed into the tent to find Sarah and said, "Quick! Grab three cups of our best flour, knead it, and make some bread."
[EN5 7-8] Then Abraham sprinted to the cattle pen, picked out a super plump calf, and handed it to his servant who got it ready, like, instantly. He then grabbed some cheese curds and milk, and brought them out with the roasted calf. He set the whole meal in front of the guys and just stood there under the tree while they ate.
[EN6 None] § A Mind-Blowing Promise
[EN7 9] The men asked him, "So, where's your wife, Sarah?"
[EN8 9] He said, "She's in the tent."
[EN9 10] One of them said, "I'm gonna come back around this time next year. And when I do, your wife Sarah will have a son." Sarah was totally eavesdropping from the tent opening, right behind the guy.
[EN10 11-12] Now, Abraham and Sarah were super old by this point, like, ancient. Sarah was way past the age of having babies. So, Sarah just laughed to herself, thinking, "Seriously? An old lady like me? Get pregnant? With this old dude as my husband?"
[EN11 13-14] Then God said to Abraham, "Why did Sarah laugh and say, 'Me? Have a baby? An old woman like me?' Is anything, like, actually too hard for God? I'll be back around this time next year, and Sarah will have a baby."
[EN12 15] Sarah totally lied. She said, "I didn't laugh," 'cause she was scared.
[EN13 15] But he was like, "Yeah, you did. You laughed."
[EN14 None] § Abraham's Epic Bargain
[EN15 16] When the men got up to leave, they headed towards Sodom. Abraham walked with them to see them off.
[EN16 17-19] Then God thought, "Should I really hide what I'm about to do from Abraham? Abraham's gonna become this huge, strong nation, and all the nations on Earth are gonna be blessed because of him. Yeah, I've chosen him to teach his kids and future family to live God's way—to be kind, generous, and fair. That way, I can do for Abraham everything I promised him."
[EN17 20-21] God went on, "The cries from Sodom and Gomorrah are getting crazy loud; the sin in those cities is off the charts. I'm going down there to see for myself if it's as bad as everyone's saying. Then I'll know for sure."
[EN18 22] The men took off for Sodom, but Abraham stood right in God's way, blocking him.
[EN19 23-25] Abraham was like, "Are you for real? Are you really gonna wipe out the good people with the bad? What if there are, like, fifty good people left in the city? Are you just gonna lump them all together and destroy everything? Wouldn't you spare the city for those fifty innocent people? I can't even believe you'd do that, killing the good and the bad like they're the same. Doesn't the Judge of the whole Earth have to be fair?"
[EN20 26] God said, "If I find fifty good people in the city of Sodom, I'll spare the whole place just for them."
[EN21 27-28] Abraham came back with, "Look, I know I'm just a guy made of dust, but can I say one more thing to my Master? What if there are only forty-five good people, not fifty? Would you destroy the city just because of five missing people?"
[EN22 28] He said, "I won't destroy it if there are forty-five."
[EN23 29] Abraham tried again, "What if you only find forty?"
[EN24 29] "I won't destroy it for forty either."
[EN25 30] He said, "Master, please don't get mad at me, but what if only thirty are found?"
[EN26 30] "Nope, I won't do it if I find thirty."
[EN27 31] He kept going, "I know I'm really testing your patience, Master, but what about for twenty?"
[EN28 31] "I won't destroy it for twenty."
[EN29 32] He just wouldn't give up. "Don't get angry, Master—this is the last time, I swear. What if you only find ten?"
[EN30 32] "For the sake of just ten, I won't destroy the city."
[EN31 33] When God finished talking with Abraham, he left. And Abraham went home.

[MSG0 18] 1-2 God appeared to Abraham at the Oaks of Mamre while he was sitting at the entrance of his tent. It was the hottest part of the day. He looked up and saw three men standing. He ran from his tent to greet them and bowed before them.
[MSG1 3-5] He said, “Master, if it please you, stop for a while with your servant. I’ll get some water so you can wash your feet. Rest under this tree. I’ll get some food to refresh you on your way, since your travels have brought you across my path.” They said, “Certainly. Go ahead.”
[MSG2 6] Abraham hurried into the tent to Sarah. He said, “Hurry. Get three cups of our best flour; knead it and make bread.”
[MSG3 7-8] Then Abraham ran to the cattle pen and picked out a nice plump calf and gave it to the servant who lost no time getting it ready. Then he got curds and milk, brought them with the calf that had been roasted, set the meal before the men, and stood there under the tree while they ate.
[MSG4 9] The men said to him, “Where is Sarah your wife?” He said, “In the tent.”
[MSG5 10] One of them said, “I’m coming back about this time next year. When I arrive, your wife Sarah will have a son.” Sarah was listening at the tent opening, just behind the man.
[MSG6 11-12] Abraham and Sarah were old by this time, very old. Sarah was far past the age for having babies. Sarah laughed within herself, “An old woman like me? Get pregnant? With this old man of a husband?”
[MSG7 13-14] God said to Abraham, “Why did Sarah laugh saying, ‘Me? Have a baby? An old woman like me?’ Is anything too hard for God? I’ll be back about this time next year and Sarah will have a baby.”
[MSG8 15] Sarah lied. She said, “I didn’t laugh,” because she was afraid. But he said, “Yes you did; you laughed.”
[MSG9 16] When the men got up to leave, they set off for Sodom. Abraham walked with them to say good-bye.
[MSG10 17-19] Then God said, “Shall I keep back from Abraham what I’m about to do? Abraham is going to become a large and strong nation; all the nations of the world are going to find themselves blessed through him. Yes, I’ve settled on him as the one to train his children and future family to observe God’s way of life, live kindly and generously and fairly, so that God can complete in Abraham what he promised him.”
[MSG11 20-21] God continued, “The cries of the victims in Sodom and Gomorrah are deafening; the sin of those cities is immense. I’m going down to see for myself, see if what they’re doing is as bad as it sounds. Then I’ll know.”
[MSG12 22] The men set out for Sodom, but Abraham stood in God’s path, blocking his way.
[MSG13 23-25] Abraham confronted him, “Are you serious? Are you planning on getting rid of the good people right along with the bad? What if there are fifty decent people left in the city; will you lump the good with the bad and get rid of the lot? Wouldn’t you spare the city for the sake of those fifty innocents? I can’t believe you’d do that, kill off the good and the bad alike as if there were no difference between them. Doesn’t the Judge of all the Earth judge with justice?”
[MSG14 26] God said, “If I find fifty decent people in the city of Sodom, I’ll spare the place just for them.”
[MSG15 27-28] Abraham came back, “Do I, a mere mortal made from a handful of dirt, dare open my mouth again to my Master? What if the fifty fall short by five—would you destroy the city because of those missing five?” He said, “I won’t destroy it if there are forty-five.”
[MSG16 29] Abraham spoke up again, “What if you only find forty?” “Neither will I destroy it if for forty.”
[MSG17 30] He said, “Master, don’t be irritated with me, but what if only thirty are found?” “No, I won’t do it if I find thirty.”
[MSG18 31] He pushed on, “I know I’m trying your patience, Master, but how about for twenty?” “I won’t destroy it for twenty.”
[MSG19 32] He wouldn’t quit, “Don’t get angry, Master—this is the last time. What if you only come up with ten?” “For the sake of only ten, I won’t destroy the city.”
[MSG20 33] When God finished talking with Abraham, he left. And Abraham went home.
