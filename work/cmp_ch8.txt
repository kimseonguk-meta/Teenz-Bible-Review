===== CHAPTER 8: A Fresh Start After the Great Flood (12 EN paras, 10 MSG paras) =====
[EN0 1-3] Then God finally remembered Noah and all the wild and farm animals with him on the ark. He sent a wind to blow across the earth, and the floodwaters started to go down. The underground springs got plugged up, the sky-windows closed, and the rain stopped. Little by little, the water level dropped. After 150 days, the worst was over.
[EN1 None] §The Water Goes Down
[EN2 4-5] On the 17th day of the 7th month, the ark landed on the mountains of Ararat. The water kept receding until the 10th month, and on the first day of that month, the tops of the mountains finally appeared. Forty days later, Noah opened the window he had made in the ark.
[EN3 6-9] He sent out a raven, which just flew back and forth until the water on the earth had dried up. Then he sent out a dove to see if the water had gone down, but the dove couldn't find a single spot to land because water still covered everything. So it came back, and Noah reached out, caught it, and brought it back inside.
[EN4 10-12] He waited another seven days and sent the dove out again. This time, it came back in the evening holding a freshly picked olive leaf in its beak. That's how Noah knew the water was almost gone. He waited another seven days and sent the dove out a third time. This time, it didn't come back.
[EN5 None] §A Whole New World
[EN6 13-14] By the time Noah was 601 years old, on the first day of the first month, the flood on the earth had dried up. Noah opened the cover of the ark and saw dry ground. By the 27th day of the second month, the earth was completely dry.
[EN7 15-17] Then God said to Noah, “Alright, time to get out of the ark—you, your wife, your sons, and their wives. Bring out every living creature with you—the birds, the animals, and all the critters that crawl on the ground. Let them go nuts and have tons of babies and fill the earth.”
[EN8 18-19] So Noah got out, along with his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives. All the animals, all the crawling creatures, and all the birds—everything that moves on the earth—left the ark, family by family.
[EN9 None] §A Promise from God
[EN10 20-21] Noah built an altar for God and took some of all the clean animals and clean birds and offered them as burnt offerings on the altar. When God smelled the sweet aroma, he thought to himself, “I’m never going to curse the ground again because of humans, even though their hearts are kinda bent toward evil from the time they're young. And I’ll never again destroy every living thing as I’ve just done.
[EN11 22] As long as the earth exists, there will always be planting and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night. That will never stop.”

[MSG3 10-11] He waited seven more days and sent out the dove again. It came back in the evening with a freshly picked olive leaf in its beak. Noah knew that the flood was about finished.
[MSG4 12] He waited another seven days and sent the dove out a third time. This time it didn’t come back.
[MSG5 13-14] In the six-hundred-first year of Noah’s life, on the first day of the first month, the flood had dried up. Noah opened the hatch of the ship and saw dry ground. By the twenty-seventh day of the second month, the Earth was completely dry.
[MSG6 15-17] God spoke to Noah: “Leave the ship, you and your wife and your sons and your sons’ wives. And take all the animals with you, the whole menagerie of birds and mammals and crawling creatures, all that swarming extravagance of life, so they can reproduce and flourish on the Earth.”
[MSG7 18-19] Noah disembarked with his sons and wife and his sons’ wives. Then all the animals, crawling creatures, birds—every creature on the face of the Earth—left the ship family by family.

God smelled the sweet fragrance and thought to himself, “I’ll never again curse the ground because of people. I know they have this bent toward evil from an early age, but I’ll never again kill off everything living as I’ve just done.
