===== CHAPTER 6: God's Epic Reset and Noah's Big Project (16 EN paras, 12 MSG paras) =====
[EN0 None] § The World Goes Wild
[EN1 1-2] So, people started spreading all over the planet, and a ton of girls were born. Then, the "sons of God" saw how beautiful the human girls were and just started picking out wives for themselves.
[EN2 3] But God was like, "Yo, I'm not gonna keep people alive forever. They're mortal, and from now on, they'll only live about 120 years."
[EN3 4] Back then, there were actual giants roaming the Earth. These giants were the kids of the "sons of God" and human women. They were like the legendary heroes you hear about in old stories, super famous and all that.
[EN4 None] § God's Regret
[EN5 5-6] God looked down and saw that things were getting seriously out of control. People were just thinking up and doing messed-up stuff 24/7. It got so bad that God was actually sorry he ever made humans. It totally broke his heart.
[EN6 7] So God said, "That's it, I'm wiping everything out. I'm hitting the fresh start on my whole creation—people, animals, bugs, birds, everything. I'm so done with all of it."
[EN7 8] But Noah was different. God saw something in Noah that he liked.
[EN8 None] § Noah Gets a Mission
[EN9 9-10] Here's the deal with Noah: he was a genuinely good guy, totally solid in his community. He was tight with God. Noah had three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
[EN10 11-12] From God's perspective, the world had turned into a total dumpster fire. Violence was everywhere. God took one look and saw how messed up everything was. Everyone was corrupt, and it was like the whole world was rotten to the core.
[EN11 13] So God told Noah, "It's the end for humanity. The world is a violent mess, and I'm about to do a total wipeout.
[EN12 14-16] "I want you to build a huge boat out of teakwood. Make a bunch of rooms in it and cover the whole thing with tar, inside and out. Make it 450 feet long, 75 feet wide, and 45 feet high. Put a roof on it with an 18-inch window near the top. Add a door on the side and build three decks—a lower, middle, and upper level.
[EN13 17] "Listen up, because I'm about to flood the entire Earth. Everything and everyone is going to be destroyed. Total annihilation.
[EN14 18-21] "But I'm making a deal with you. You're going to get on that boat with your wife, your sons, and their wives. And you need to bring two of every living creature—a male and a female—on the boat with you to keep them safe. Two of every kind of bird, mammal, and reptile. You've gotta save them. Also, grab a ton of food, enough for you and all the animals."
[EN15 22] And Noah did it. He did everything exactly as God told him to.
